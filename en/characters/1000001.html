Story

First meet

Mu Yifeng, the senior of the Cloud Spirit sword sect, enter the sect when he was young, the master of the cloud sword, and reached the natural realm. Usually, he practices the sword in the sect with the black birds and mist between the mountains. There is a hearsay that the sword way he created was inspired by this. His sword is elegant and agile. Normally he likes to collect the mist to make tea and is not talkative. He usually uses tea to entertain friends and treats others friendly but strictly to himself. He always goes all out for the sect. There's nearly no one who knows the true meaning of the Quechen-sword given by their suzerain and never sees it out of its sheath.

The Black Bird

Many years after the ancient demon Yao invasion into the Lianyun mountain and was killed, although the rain came to wash and pollute the dirt, there were times that raged with venomous insects, gnawed on the fields and harming the country. There were birds flying from the cold land in the north, tails like scissors, bit mud into the mountains. Only a few years later, there were birds like these flying sideways in the countries to look for food, villages have venomous insects of less harm, and people called them Black Birds.

When MU Yifeng was young, he was practicing swordsmanship in the mountains. When he watched the Black Birds rise and gall, saw they were nimble and comprehended a set of various bodily movement skills. When his peers competed with Yifeng, they all called him nimble. During the competition, before the edge of the sword touched his sleeve, the figure of Yifeng had already flashed behind him.

In his spare time, Yifeng often sat at the top of a tree for rest. The Black Birds saw him as elegant and quiet, never moving, so they often played beside Yifeng. Seeing that the Black Birds were so naive and helpful to the villagers, Yifeng was very happy, so he would occasionally catch venomous insects and clouds to feed them. As time went by, the Black Birds would often accompany him.

Cloud and Mist

The main peak of Cloud Spirit Clan is more than a thousand zhangs high, often accompanied by a sea of clouds. When the sea of clouds rain, in the mountains into a stream. Streams from the cliff washed down and the cloud, cloud was originally from immortals, and gather the Qi of three continents. After drinking it, the inner spirit was quiet, its Dantian field was full of energy and the Dao Mindset is solid.

When Mu Yifeng practiced swords in the mountains, he often felt that the clouds were unpredictable in the mountains, just like the sect's sword technique. After the suzerain's guidance, lead this cloud changes into the sword move, then Mu Yifeng is still young but the cloud sword had acquired great achievement. Yifeng has practiced dharma for several years and led the clouds and mists into the body. Between his rhythmic breathing, the cloud and mist will phantom into his figures.

The mist is the spring, and forms spiritual water. Whenever the master has to go out, Mu Yifeng often prepared a pot of cloud spring water, collected some good tea on the way to the market, and cook it with the cloud spring water. Drink it, he could not eat for a few days and was not hungry, in addition to the power way. A poem said: Birds set off thousands of miles high, sword flashing, wind singing, and the sea of cloud turning. The great truth only concerns itself, but the Quechen-sword will settle a grudge in the world.

The Quechen-sword

This sword was forged by Lu who lives in the north, the material is millennium-cold iron. It was more than four feet and two inches long. Its body and hilt were in one, and all of them were snow-white and free from dust. The hilt of the sword was inlaid with the evil dispelling stone, and the head of the sword was carved with a red ear of blue jade. The scabbard was taken from the heart of the black sandalwood tree on the south side of the sea and was engraved with ten thousand runes sect's spirit protecting spell to strengthen it. The scabbard was made from the five elements mountain's black soil. After entering the Lu clan's heaven and earth refining furnace, it took forty-nine days to refine the essences, which were embedded in the sword, The black one was possessed by the cloud; the yellow one turned into the earth and carried things; the cloud moved with the wind; the gold one has reinforced the base; the sword was free from dust out of the earth. It was called the dust sword and was given to the cloud spirit sword sect.

Mu Yifeng's father Mu Yunren, is the sheriff of QingYan county, full of justice and not afraid of evils, making QingYan county safe and peaceful for a long time. YiXian calendar, six BingShen Year, the merfolks from the south sea driven by Fei, invaded the QingYan country. The merfolk are brutal and bloodthirsty, YunRen and his follower fight with them for several days and are defeated to die. After several days, the army from the continent arrived, only seeing the countless injured and ruins. In the meantime, the suzerain of the Cloud Spirit sword sect heard this, was moved by Yunren's charity, and found Mu Yifeng's spiritual root is unusual. Hence made the Mu Yifeng his personal disciple, rewarding him with an Quechen-sword. The suzerain hopes he can forget the disturbance in mortal life and concentrate on the Dao.

When he became the Cloud Spirit Sword Sect senior, Mu Yifeng followed the suzerain's will, cultivated his sword intent, and did not use his sword commonly. Although sometimes he faces unfairness, he holds Quechen-sword without unsheathed and uses a sheathed sword moves to bring the fair. He usually seems reticent and not easy to get close to, but everyone who has been with Mu Yifeng praises him as a kind person, with a heart for the ordinary people in the world.
