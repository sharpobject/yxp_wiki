Story

First meet

Hua Qinrui, the cultivator of Black Tortoise Rudder of the Five Elements Alliance, the young master of the Fate Hall of Dayan Kingdom.

It has been handed down from generation to generation that there is a peach tree that was blessed with good fortune, accumulated the innate water of the Five Elements Tree and Xuanming Pool, and gained the popularity of the Fate Hall. A baby girl was born from the fruit of the peach tree. The baby girl was raised by Hua Ruqing, the master of Fate Hall, and named Qinrui. Later, Hua Qinrui joined the Five Elements Alliance, and obtained the Peach Branch Ruyi, which was transformed from the branches of the same peach tree.

Hua Qinrui is usually lively and bright, inherits the purpose of Fate Hall, and likes to help fellow Daoists to fall into the fate of love.

Celestial Peach

A hundred years after the shattering of the Great Rules, the three continents have just begun to recover. There is a fine peach pit with purple veins that came from the crack of the Great Rules, took the wind to Three Continents, and landed in the Five Elements Mountain. The peach pit didn't have a good growing environment at first, so it didn't germinate, but it was protected by the five-element tree, so it didn't rot. After thousands of years, the five-element tree grows, and a pond grows at the bottom of the tree. The peach pit germinates after getting the water of the five elements tree, and it only takes a few days to grow into a big tree, and it only takes a few years for the peach tree to become a peach forest.

Later, Hua Ruyu, the master of Jinniu Hall of Black Tortoise Rudder, saw that the water spirit in this peach forest is mellow and can help practice. So she picked up the Qiankun Snail and put a whole peach tree into it. Afterwards, it was transported to Black Tortoise Rudder of East Continent, and the peach tree was placed beside Xuanming Pool. The peach tree gets Xuanming water, the flowers and leaves are slim, and the vitality is flourishing. After several years, the peach tree feels the spiritual flow by the pool, the branches of the peach tree move with the air, the branches and leaves of the peach tree bloom, and the tree is full of flowers and fruits. Thanks to the peach tree's help, Black Tortoise Rudder's middle Qi flourished, and the number of cultivators gradually increased. Later, when cultivators were meditating by the pool, they would see the peach tree nodding and shaking its heads, and hear the sound of the wind ringing in their ears. It seems that the peach tree have produced spiritual wisdom, and it want to talk to people. According to legend, Huaqin stamens are born from this peach tree.

Peach Branch Ruyi

The branches of the peach tree are as hard as a rock.

A hundred years ago, a peach branch fell on the surface of Xuanming Pool. Hua Ruyu saw Qi in it, and wanted to refine it into a magic weapon. So she ordered the disciples to use gold axes, gold chisels, gold planes, and gold shovels to transform the peach branch, and the shape of the peach branch remained unchanged. After nine days, there was only peach gum all over the ground. Some disciples collected peach gum, soaked it in the water of Xuanming Pool and drank it. It tasted like fine nectar. After drinking, the disciples felt that the wood spirit gathered in the dantian. Everyone tried to transform the peach branches again, but the peach branches remained unchanged. Hua Ruyu saw that the peach branch was indestructible and could not be used by herself, so she wanted to discard it, but felt it was a pity, so she covered it with soil and ignored it.

Later Hua Qinrui turned into a human being, and came from the same root as the peach branch, and they have mutual affection. When Hua Qinrui returned to Black Tortoise Rudder for the first time, the peach branch broke through the ground and turned into a Ruyi in the air, wrapping around Hua Qinrui's side, unwilling to go for a long time. Hua Qinrui has accumulated the innate water of the Five Elements Tree and Xuanming Pool, and now she has this Peach Branch Ruyi, which gathers the spiritual energy of the wood. With the water and wood, Hua Qinrui's Qi can operate very efficiently.

Fate Hall

Bat Demon Wu and Hua Ruyu's younger brother Hua Ruqing once practiced Taoism in the Black Tortoise Rudder Nutu Hall of the Five Elements Alliance. They have been in love for many years and became a Taoist couple. One day, Wu and Hua Ruqing discussed the Tao under the moonlight, feeling that everything is destined, but most of them can't get it because time doesn't wait. Wu and Hua Ruqin communicated to the depths of their souls, and both are willing to spend their lives to fulfill the fate of all things. Thus, one person and one demon, abandoned many years of cultivation, went to Dayan Kingdom, created the Fate Hall, and fulfilled the predestined relationship of all the people in the world.

One year, when the Qixi Festival was approaching, Hua Ruqing traveled all over the three continents, looking for auspicious things to decorate the hall. When he arrived at Black Tortoise Rudder, Hua Ruqing saw that the tree was full of peach blossoms, lively and cute, so he begged his sister for a flower. Hua Ruyu only said that this tree is invincible, if the younger brother is capable, he can take it by himself. Hua Ruqing sat under the peach tree for a long time, and said: The peach tree is slender and sturdy, Flaming red are its blossoms. The girl is getting married, Most suitable for the house. Suddenly, the wind blows, blowing a branch down, falling In Hua Ruqing's arms.

Hua Ruqing got the peach blossom branch, put it in a jade bottle, and placed it in the center of Fate Hall. Later, whether they were cultivators or mortals, most of the people who came and went to Fate Hall were humans. Under the influence of human beings, Peach Blossom has become more and more lively. It can make its own voice, like a whisper. One day, the flowers close and the sound stops. Both Wu and Hua Ruqing were very surprised. They tried all kinds of methods but the flowers could not bloom again. In mid-autumn, the flowers bloom again, with little fluorescent lights, accompanied by purple clouds, and a baby girl appears in the heart of the flowers.

Hua Ruqing was very happy to have a daughter, named Qinrui, and raised her with Wu. When Hua Qinrui was young, she liked to chat with the visitors of Fate Hall, and often made marriages between the visitors through talking and laughing, as if she was born for this. Later, when Hua Qinrui was a little older, she seemed quite mischievous. Her parents hoped that she would cultivate her morality, so she begged Hua Ruyu to accept her as a disciple. When Hua Qinrui practiced the water spirit technique, her aura seemed natural, and she could easily perform ordinary spells. Hua Qinrui also likes to play, and often teases her peers in the rudder with spells. Cultivators of the same generation couldn't bear to blame Hua Qinrui for her innocence, and after a long time, they all started playing with her. Later, the atmosphere of Black Tortoise Rudder was quite different from that of ordinary cultivation sects.
