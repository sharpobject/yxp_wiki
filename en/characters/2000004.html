Story

First meet

The Jiang family is a well-known family on the three continents. There are many cultivators in the clan, and many people enter the Heptastar Pavilion. Jiang Ximing entered the Heptastar Pavilion when he was a child, inherited his father's arts, and was taught by the pavilion master himself, and his practice far surpassed his peers. It is hearsay that when he was born, he was accompanied by a white deer, and his hair gradually turned white due to the Qi of the white deer. He has an innate sense of the power of the stars. After several years of practice, he has gradually developed the ability of the stars. He has repeatedly made extraordinary achievements for the Heptastar Pavilion. He often manages the big and small things in the pavilion, watches the stars every night, and plans strategies.

Star River Deer Envoy

Jiang Jin, the descendant of Jiang Shang who is the first master of Heptastar Pavilion, inherited his ancestor's career and got the help of the Liushu Pavilion and the Ten Thousand Runes Sect. Using the spirit stone as the source and the sacred tree as the axis, he drew ten thousand runes to draw the stars' power and drove the pavilions into the sky. Spread Trigrams on three continents. And he uses eight words to make eight seniorities in the family which are: Jin Hou Wei Feng, Zhong Ren Xi Yi. He hopes that future generations even if they have learned Trigrams but self-cultivation is always the most important thing and be righteous and help mortal people avoid disasters and seek blessings. Therefore, many descendants of the Jiang family entered the Heptastar Pavilion, many of whom were officials of the Dayan kingdom.

Jiang Ximing's father, Jiang Renwei, was an outstanding disciple of the Heptastar Pavilion, Jiang Jin personally taught him, and he was the astrologer. In the five GuiHai years of the Yi Xian calendar, Jiang Renwei was ordered to investigate the matter of Angu refining and transforming evil spirits. Unfortunately, he was injured by an attack by evil spirits. He fled to Wuge Mountain and was rescued by Lan Yuxin. Afterward, the two falls in love with each other and they are not greedy for power and wealth, play the zither with each other. Finally, they go into the deep forest to live in seclusion for several years and born Jiang Ximing. It is said that the stars are shining at night, and a white deer came to the depths of the forest. It hummed in front of the hut for a long time and refused to go. After growing up with Jiang Ximing, Jiang Ximing was affected by the Qi of the white deer, and his hair gradually turned white and his Qi is pure and long. His father taught him the Heptastar arts, and his practice is far superior to his peers.

After entering the Heptastar Pavilion, Jiang Ximing's power gradually developed, and he made outstanding achievements repeatedly. Then the son inherited his father's career and was promoted to astrologer. When people saw him often riding a white deer, they called him the Star River Deer Envoy.

Heptastar Ruler

The scriptures say: The Big Dipper, the great virtues of the heaven and the earth, the true righteousness and the formation of the mysterious image, transported to the mid-heaven, established four seasons, equalized the five elements, lived and killed all things, ruled the heaven and the earth, checked and recorded good and evil, and nothing is inseparable. It is in charge of everything.

The artifact in the Heptastar Pavilion which shaped like a ruler so-called Heptaster Ruler. It can be used in divination or observing the stars. It takes the root of the five-element tree, soaks in the water from the source of Mount Tai for a hundred years, and burns it into carbon, and the mixed spirit stone is buried in the soil for a hundred years and turns into gold it inheriting the law of mutual generation of all thing in the three continents and it is to think that as Kun. And then take seven meteorites, hang them in the Heptastar Pavilion, use the wind and rain to drench them, and wait for a hundred years to become jade naturally, gathering the Qi of the sky and it is to think as Qian. Hence the ruler has the power of Qian-Kun which means the sky and the earth. Qi measures The Big Dipper and develops the general trend of the three continents. Those who hold a ruler to climb high and measure the stars have all the flow of Qi in their hands. They can control the pavilions near them and hold the stars far away.

The master of the pavilion thinks Jiang Ximing is good at the power of the stars young and steady and gave him the Heptastar Ruler. Since then, the movement of the Heptastar Pavilion on three continents has been controlled by Jiang Ximing.

Strategize

The scriptures say: Likewise, the Holy One is good at saving people, And no one is abandoned. He is good at saving all things, And nothing is abandoned. This is called following the light.

When Jiang Ximing was born, his parents made great wishes for him to become a saint. When he was a child, the two elders entrusted him to the Heptastar Pavilion, and lived in seclusion for many years. Jiang Ximing rarely gets together with his family and is usually taken care of by the elders in the pavilion. Compared with his parents, Jiang Ximing's white deer beside him is close at every step, which is better than his relatives.

Life and practice in the Heptastar Pavilion are heavy and not very interesting. All the elders encourage the disciples to experience in the world to practice more in their spare time, to practice divination, to increase their knowledge, and to do more good deeds. There is no definite location for the Heptastar group pavilions, and where the Qi is drawn, the pavilion can move across the three continents easily. For several years, Jiang Ximing traveled to three continents with the pavilions, and the world was amazed to see him riding a white deer. Every time someone came up to ask things, Jiang Ximing would answer all kinds of questions. When meeting mortals, he would answer questions and solve doubts about the ominous or auspicious of divination. When he meet a cultivator, he responded to every request from medicine to dharma, and his heart was very sincere. He was always like this, and many acquaintances all communicated with Heptastar Pavilion in the name of Jiang Ximing, so he was very famous at a young age.

In the year of Jiang Ximing's thirties, he was promoted to be an astrologer, but he was less and less likely to experience in the world. Usually, when he stays in the Pavilion, he records the big and small affairs of the Pavilion, watches the stars move at night, moves the position of the Pavilion, set sail, holds the Heptastar Ruler, and gives orders to everyone. And when he goes out, he is busier with the affairs of the Pavilion. Sending disciples to the uncharted realm, marketing in mortal's market, communicating with other sects or sending disciples to fight with the devils, he all rides on a white deer and returns quickly when he is done, it is not like wandering in the state of mind when he were young. The disciples saw that Jiang Ximing often stayed in the pavilion, but managed everything in an orderly manner. They all said that the Star River Deer Envoy was a strategist who planned a strategy and won a decisive victory thousands of miles away. However, Jiang Ximing held an important position, there are only white deer around who can express their hearts, so the seekers are few, and the road of saints is lonely.
