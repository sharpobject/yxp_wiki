Story

First meet

Wu Ce, the child of Wu Heng. Wu Heng, the right general of the North Wind Army. When Wu Ce was young, Jiang Ximing gave Wu Ce a Star Moon Folding Fan, and also taught Wu Ce the cycle of Qi. Later, Wu Ce grew up to be good at stargazing and scheming.

Wu Ce once was the military advisor of Wu Heng Army and achieved many successes in battle. However, Wu Ce was jealous by the superior because of Wu Heng Army's high achievements, and Wu Ce was set up by the superior and disappeared. There is a rumor that Wu Ce was killed by devils, and there is also a rumor that Wu Ce has entered the Seven Stars Pavilion, and often be seen in the East Coast of North Continent.

Star Moon Folding Fan

The eastern part of the North Continent is connected to the Sea, and the North Wind Army has set up the Sea Filling Camp near the coast, under the shelter of the Sea Filling Camp, the nearby villages are mostly engaged in fishing. During the daytime, there is often a fleet of ships patrolling near the shore. Thousands of sails move, ten thousand miles of clouds are divided, the wind is sluggish and snowy, and the water is tantalizing and turbulent. Every night, the fishing fires are brilliant, the Big Dipper appendages are slanted upward, the cooking smoke is gathered near, and the stars are listed in the distance.

Wu Ce's father, Wu Heng, was the deputy general of the North Wind Army at that time, good at sea warfare, and had a private army of one hundred men. YiXian calendar, six JiHai year, Jiang Ximing helped Wu Heng's army to exterminate the evil in the eastern seashore, and saw the young Wu Ce often lying on the top of the room of the houseboat, looking up and watching the stars. Jiang was curious, so he went up to test Wu Ce's astrological knowledge and found that Wu Ce answered all the questions correctly. Later, Jiang Ximing calculated Wu Ce's fate, nodded his head to express his approval, and then gave Wu Ce a folding fan and taught Wu Ce dozens of phrases of the Qi circulation, and Wu Ce listened and repeated them without missing a word. Later, before Jiang Ximing left, he told Wu Heng that this child had a immortal's fate, so he could always bring the child around to keep him safe.

After nightfall, the sea is occasionally covered with miasma. Whenever the miasma is thick, it is like a gray veil to block the eyes. If a ship sails into the deep sea at this time, it will be invaded by the demonic Qi and few people will survive. Wu Ce often accompanies father to sail at night, holding a folding fan and navigating. When Wu Ce closes the fan, Qi can be gathered in the dantian, and the power of the stars and the moon can be felt when the Qi flows. When Wu Ce opens the fan, the Qi is dispersed on the side of Wu Ce, and when Wu Ce swings the fan, vitality of heaven and earth can be seen. With Wu Ce's fan dance, the moon and stars shone brightly, and the miasma was dispersed. All the ships travel with Wu Ce never lost their voyage.

Dipper Interlocking

Most of the children on the East Coast like to play with crabs and waves, build sand and collect shells. Wu Ce did not enjoy this, but only enjoyed looking at the stars and navigating for the army. At other times, Wu Ce often stayed in father's camp, listening to the deliberations and thinking. Besides listening to the deliberations, Wu Ce also likes to read military books, the speed of reading is very fast, in less than a few months, there is no book in the camp that Wu Ce has not yet read.

Later, Wu Ce observed father and others' deliberations, and when they discussed the war, Wu Ce might have something to say. At first heard Wu Ce's words, the people were sulking with the child's presumptuous words, and wanted to reprimand Wu Ce but feared Wu Heng's majesty, while Wu Heng also had a look of displeasure. However, after thinking about Wu Ce's plan, everyone went silent. After a while, Wu Heng said with a big laugh:" since no one can criticize Ce's speech, that is, Ce's plan is also acceptable, we will act on this plan, okay?" So Wu's army acted on Wu Ce's plan and actually gained the victory.

After a few years, Wu Ce gradually became a strategic advisor, and Wu Heng's army was entrusted to Wu Ce on all matters of importance. Normally, Wu Ce would accompany father to patrol the camp and the soldier met during the patrol, Wu Ce would remember his name and appearance, then if Wu Ce met the soldier again, Wu Ce would be able to call his name, and if the soldier had difficulties, Wu Ce could also advise him. After a long time, the soldiers all take Wu Ce as their relative. Wu Ce also taught the soldiers the method of qi circulation, and later the soldiers marched according to Wu Ce's method and were rarely disturbed by the demonic qi.

In order to help the army, Wu Ce purchased devices from Tianji Valley, referring to the direction of the stars to install the devices between the fleets. The formation formed by the fleet is called the Dipper Interlocking Formation. According to this formation, the navy uses devices as the communication tool, sailing into the sea without fear of dispersion, and the ships look after each other, so ordinary devils cannot be a threat. Because Wu Ce is good at divination and can determine the direction of the wind, the soldiers often build fires upwind according to Wu Ce's dispatch to drive away the devils. Fire on the shore, and ships in the sea, together they attacked the devils, and for several years, the devils on the east coast gradually became less and less.

Act Underhand

At that time, the right general Wang Shenyu was in charge of the whole army of Sea Filling Camp. Seeing Wu Heng's repeated successes in recent years, he felt that Wu Heng had a tendency to surpass himself in terms of fame and was not happy for a long time.

One day, the scouts reported that there were demons gathering outside of the Cold Pass, and Wang Jian Yu ordered Wu Heng's army to abandon the ship and take the land route out of the Cold Pass to expel the demons. After receiving the order, Wu Heng fell into silence and did not take any action. After Wu Ce divined the fate, and also wept in silence. On the next day, with the breeze blowing in the north, Wu Heng and his soldiers drank hot wine together and went out of the Cold Pass.

A few miles to the devil group, Wu Ce led troops to dig a pit and set up an ambush, and set up fire soldiers on the other three sides of the devil group. That night, Wu Ce drew the power of the stars and turned the wind to the north. Wu Heng ordered the bow soldiers to shoot at the devils, while the other three sides of the devils were surrounded by blazing fire. The devils were frightened and ran to the north, and for a while a large number of devils were trampled and burned, and the number of devils was greatly reduced. After a while, the fire weakened a little, some devils turned to the south to attack, and they all entered the trap set by Wu Ce. When the trap was full, Wu Heng led his infantry to fight and move slowly southward while fighting, and when they ran out of arrows, they had already reached the bottom of the Cold Pass. However, Wang Shenyu stood at the high wall and closed the gate tightly with the excuse that the devils were not yet exhausted, and not even release arrows to help. Wu Heng's army rushed out and gradually disappeared in the wind and snow of the North Continent.

A few days later, the devil group dispersed, Wu Heng army back to camp. The number of soldiers was large when they left the camp, but when they returned, there were not even a hundred left. Wu Heng pushed a wooden cart, covered with his cloak, followed by the soldiers all have angry and sad look. Wu Heng wrapped his cloak around the body on the cart and put it on a boat, and all the soldiers wept and sent the boat into the sea.

After a few months, Wang Shenyu felt that Wu Ce had been eliminated and Wu Heng was depressed and ignoring military affairs, so he had nothing to worry about in his position as general. However, during the next few months, the troops were often assisted by a mysterious person who wore Wu Heng's cloak and always appeared when there was an imminent crisis, but left as soon as the crisis was over. The soldiers talked privately that it was Wu Ce's ghost who was vowing to take revenge. When Wang Shenyu heard this, he was furious and persecuted the soldiers who had spread the rumors. Gradually, the number of persecuted soldiers increased, and the army was filled with discontent. A discontented soldier even drunkenly assassinated Wang Shenyu, but was unsuccessful.

The following night, the star fell. The wind blew Wang Shenyu's tent, Wang Shenyu woke up, went out of the tent, and saw Wu Ce standing straight in front of him, Wang Shenyu was shocked, waving his sword at Wu Ce, the sword penetrated Wu Ce's body and passed, as if there was nothing to cut, Wang Shenyu had to shout for help. Seeing all the soldiers out of the tent, Wu Ce rebuked Wang Shenyu's faint, incompetent and shameless, saying that he was only good at framing loyalty, wasting military pay, and really is the scourge of the three continents, everyone would like to kill Wang Shenyu for justice. After hearing this, Wang Shenyu's anger attacked his heart and his wounds burst open before he died. When the soldiers came back to their senses, they no longer knew where Wu Ce had gone.

Later, the commander of the North Wind Army received a letter from the Heptastar Pavilion, praising Wu Heng's loyalty and bravery, and Wu Heng was promoted to the right general, in charge of the whole army of Sea Filling Camp.
