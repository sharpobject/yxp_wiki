Story

First meet

Ji Fangsheng, Duan Xuan Sect's Senior Brother. He usually seldom shows his face, and the number of people who have seen Ji Fangsheng is even fewer. It seems to be a person who lives only in the rumors. There are rumors that his appearance is unchanged for a hundred years, and his cultivation level is unmeasurable, but also rumors that his cultivation level is very low, and it doesn't feel any pain when he hits people.

Zhe Cang (Hiding)

Currently, the most deeply hidden sect in the three continents is the Duan Xuan Sect, and the most reclusive cultivator of the Duan Xuan Sect is none other than Ji Fangsheng.

Among the disciples of the Duan Xuan Sect, from the time they started practicing to the time they passed away, there were many who had never seen Ji Fang Sheng, and even more who wondered whether Fangsheng existed or not.

Among the disciples, there is a rumor that the master Yuan Shi of Gui Zhi Cave likes to tech gifted ones, and if he sees any good talents among the disciples, he will send Ji Fang Sheng to guide them. The disciples who were guided by Ji Fangsheng were started early or late, spanning more than a hundred years. The disciples said Ji Fangsheng's appearance, a hundred years actually did not change the slightest, all say he is a handsome young man . However, when people wanted to see the young man's cultivation, they were unable to know.

Whenever he instructed the disciples, Ji Fangsheng's fists and palms were light, and his movement was leisurely. Disciples do not feel any pain when they are hit by his fists and palms. However, if someone's meridians do not work, Ji Fangsheng palm down, or crying, or laughing, ugly. They were hit by Ji Fangsheng's palm is like being hit by the master's ruler, their own cultivation is not home, all show in front of the Fangsheng. But when rumors are spread, their own ugly behavior is often hidden, leaving only the words of Ji Fangsheng hit people do not hurt.

It is also rumored among the cave masters that the master Yuanshi often orders Ji Fang Sheng to visit the 36 caves and practice. Whenever Fang Sheng went to each cave, he did not say much, just silently read the books or listened to the words of the cave masters, and he never forgot the methods and skills once he learned them.

Only a few years, the best of the 36 caves were all given to Fang Sheng. And Fang Sheng hid in the cave, not come out for many years, his cultivation gained gradually and united.

Fang Sheng (Just Born)

As early as the beginning of the Duan Xuan Sect, the cave masters searched all over the three continents, collected the world's secret books and secret manuals hidden in the caves, and merged the essence of the three religions in the Duan Xuan Sect, which was the beginning of the Duan Xuan Sect.

Later, the master Yuanshi accepted the brothers Fang Sheng and Fang Li of the Ji Clan. The two brothers were gifted with extraordinary talents, and they were the geniuses of Xuan cultivation. Fang Sheng is the elder brother, who likes to be quiet and often lives in the cave to sit in meditation. Fang Li is the younger brother, who likes to be active and travels a lot. The elder brother is better in Spirit and the younger brother is better in Physique. After a few years, the master Yuanshi saw that the two of them only chose one between Spirit and Physique, which was not conducive to their cultivation. So he ordered them to be each other's teachers and to share their knowledge with each other, so that their Spirit and Physique could be strengthened.

Fang Sheng uses Spirit to stabilize Physique, which is getting stronger and stronger. He usually resides in the cave without going out, and he has a Zen mindset when he walks, stands, sits and sleeps in the cave. When cultivating, Fang Sheng has no eyes to see, no ears to hear, no heart to know, no worries to feel, no dreams to sleep. As he cultivation for a hundred years in this way, the high level of his cultivation seems to be standing on the top of the three continents at that time.

Fang Sheng cultivation for a hundred years and the first time to feel the realm of returning to the void. Although Fang Sheng has not yet entered the realm, he is able to transform spirit by reversing void, transform Qi by reversing spirit, and transform physique by reversing Qi. So his Spirit becomes void and his Physique is strong enough to heal itself from injuries and fill up itself from damages. Where Fang Sheng has no intention of hurting anyone, the qi that rhymes with the palm can pass through the meridians and heal one's body and spirit. Sect disciples do not feel pain when they are hit by Fang Sheng's palm, instead, their practice is benefited, but the ordinary disciples are not aware of it.

Fang Li (Just Dead)

Hundreds of years ago, The Duan Xuan Sect has a lot of disciples, quite a big sect of the trend, admirers come in an endless stream. However, in terms of top level cultivation, there are only three of them, the Master Yuanshi and the Ji Brothers. During the past hundred years, the three of them have realized the Dao of Xuan, and they can feel the flow of the Qi of the three continents.

Since Ji Fangli dedicated to cultivate Spirit, he rarely go out of the sect, learning from Ji Fangsheng to sit in meditation facing the wall, and when he cultivate to the deep end, he felt the demonic Qi rampant in the innate Qi, and his cultivation could not be further progressed. After Fangli want to break the demonic Qi situation, he traveled east to the coast of Canghai, west to the top of Mount Tai, south to the end of the Hundred Rivers, north to the land of the Ten Thousand Devils. Within a few years, Fangli investigated the demonic qi in the three continents, knew that it originated from outside the three continents, and realized that its nature is far from the innate Qi - it doesn't distinguish between clear and turbid, but it contains power within, and is often hidden in all things in the three continents. Fangli introduced the demonic Qi into his dantian to try it out, only felt that his mind was greatly shaken, and fearing that it would be detrimental to his cultivation, he had to give up.

Later, Ji Fangli returned to the sect and reported what he discovered to Master Yuanshi. He said, the innate Qi of the three continents is losing, the demonic Qi is getting more and more stronger everyday, and the cultivators of the three continents will not reduce the wastage of the innate Qi, and in future, he afraid that there will be no Qi for cultivation. At that time, the demonic Qi filled the three continents, the living are all in jeopardy. In order to break this situation, they must open the source of income and cut down the expenses. In order to open the source, they should seal the source of demonic qi, so that the demonic qi will be reduced and the spiritual Qi will be increased. To cut down expenses, they need to eliminate the living beings who can sense the Qi, so that there will be fewer cultivators and more spiritual Qi. In this way, peace can be maintained in the three continents.

The master Yuanshi was furious and blamed Fangli for being tainted by the demonic Qi, and his desire to kill. As a result, Fangli wanted to leave the sect and go his own way, but master Yuanshi refused to allow him to do so, so Fangli suddenly started to attack and hurt master Yuanshi. When Fangsheng came forward to stop Fangli, the two brothers fought for a long time, but it was hard to tell the winner. Later, Fangli drew the demonic Qi into his body and his cultivation increased, and his fist and palms became extremely powerful. Fangsheng, who was thinking of his brother's bond and the righteousness of his sect, was hesitant to fight, and eventually he was defeated, and his hundred years of cultivation was broken by Fangli, and his body was destroyed and was going to perish.

Afterwards, Fangli disappeared to the Western Continent. The master Yuanshi observed Fangsheng, although Fangsheng's body was destroyed but his soul was not extinguished. Then master Yuanshi damaged his own body to save Fangsheng, so Fangsheng returned to life. Ji Fangsheng's current life is not yet finished, but he seems to have arrived in the afterlife, his memories of his past life are all shattered, and at this time, he is like a newborn child descending into the world. After this accident, the master Yuanshi ordered that the gate of Duan Xuan Sect should be forever closed, and a restriction should be laid around the mountain to cut off the communication with the outside world.
