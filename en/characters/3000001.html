Story

First meet

Wu Xingzhi, a lonely person in the world is often accompanied by wine. The Gourd Of Leisurely is around the waist, the Five-Element Spirit pattern is tattooed on the arm, the Settle the Storm is held in the hand, and just rely on those three artifacts, let him do what he want.

Since Wu joined the Five Elements Alliance, Wu has never stayed in one place for too long, and Wu has traveled all over the three continents at ease. Wu has learned all the arts of the Five Elements. When encountering injustice in the world, if he likes it, take care of it, if he doesn't like it, leave. Or encountering evil, he will try his best to kill it, and after a long time, he is very famous on three continents.

Wu has traveled extensively and has more combat experience than ordinary cultivator. Wu is good at using combat to try out methods, the longer it lasts, the more powerful it will be.

Five Elements Spirit Pattern

When Wu was young, evil spirits killed his parents, and he often begged alone in the city of Luxue. When people drove him away, he went to the reeds outside the town to dig some birds' eggs and pick some reeds to satisfy his hunger. After a long time, the sky was his teacher, and the earth his mother, weaving nets to catch fish, throwing stones to hunt birds, weaving hats, and weaving shoes, all survival skills are omnipotent.

At that time, the Five Elements Alliance set up sub-rudders in various places according to the weather conditions of the three continents. Among them, the white tiger rudder was closer to the city of Luxue, and the two areas often communicated. When he was a little older, it was convenient for him to set up a flagpole outside the city gate, and sell coolies. He often runs errands to make a living and transports some spiritual herbs and clothes for the two places. After many times he starts making some friends with those cultivators. And the cultivators in the white tiger rudder also teach him some methods of self-cultivation, those cultivators didn't know that Wu learned very quickly, and he was able to absorb Qi into his body in just a few days. The owner of the white tiger rudder found he is so talented and hurriedly invited it into the rudder. In just a few months, he has finished practicing all the dharma in the rudder. Still, he has lived in the mountains for many years, and he feels uncomfortable living in the rudder, so he resigned from the cultivator in the rudder to travel far away.

In the next few years, he traveled all over the continents of the three continents, using all the arts of the Five Elements Alliance. Then he went to the west continents to collect various spiritual ores, grind them into powder, and visit the Five Elements Mountain. He asked the water from the Five Elements Tree to mix into powder to make juice. He took animal bones and pierced the Five Elements Dharma on the arm because the five elements dharma are pierced on the arm, when he need to use the five elements trick in the battle, he doesn't need to make a seal and just use Qi to drive the spirit pattern on his arm. This will bring him a huge opportunity when he fights with others.

Gourd Of Leisurely

When Wu Xingzhi was traveling on three continents, he visited a lot of villages, but with more demons wandering around fewer people will occur. The so-called inhumanity of heaven and earth regards all things as dogs, and as far as the eye can see, there are miserable scenes everywhere. Whenever Wu Xingzhi encounters evil spirits, he will try his best.

One day, a demon called Si came from the mountain road near the sea in the eastern continent. It was more than ten feet tall and had a big arm and a round waist. It held a purple gourd on the road. There is water coming out of the gourd, and the living beings will faint after drinking it, and when they faint, they will be eaten by evil spirits and eat their marrow. As Wu traveled to this place he saw that the demon was extremely swift, grabbed his body, and poured the gourd into his mouth. When he was caught, he had no time to resist. He drank most of the water from the gourd, but he didn't feel faint. His refreshing and Qi increased greatly, so he urged the five elements and the demon to fight for a long time. When Wu Xingzhi went to grab the gourd and drink it, he became more and more courageous in the battle. The Si demon is defeated and flees by the sea. It is that the gourd is a supernatural thing, but it was obtained by Demon Si by chance, and Si doesn't know its origin, and used it to harm the three continents.

Wu Xingzhi got the gourd to observe it. There is energy inside, and when it meets water, it turns into a pulp and gives off the aroma of the drink. Wu is very happy with the gourd and he inscribed it with the five-element runes on the energy. Since then, no matter whether the water is safe or poisoned, after turning it into a drink, he can safely drink it. The clearer the drink, the purer the aroma, and the more turbid the wine, the stronger the taste, so walking in the world, it is enough to prepare a leisurely gourd.

Settle the Storm

At the beginning of Wu Xingzhi's trip to three continents, he returned to Luxue City to collect old things and demolished the old flagpole at the city gate. He met Yu Feng, the head of the city. Yu Feng saw that his behavior was vulgar and suspected that he was an outlaw, so he stepped forward to stop him. The two of them got angry when they argued, they fought together with their bare hands, and the people around hurriedly stepped forward to persuade them to leave, after some explanations, they realized that there was a misunderstanding, but one said that the art of the five elements is ready to go, and the other said weapon is still on the horse. They are all dissatisfied. The two meet for a limit of one month, and the number of devils killed is a gamble. The failed one needs to kowtow at the city gate.

For the next few days, the two rode their horses to the northern continents, further northward, killing devils during the day, lighting a fire at night, resting their horses, drinking and cooking, and chatting about how happy they were. One day, after drinking for three rounds, Yu Feng took a package on his horse's back and said: This package contains three handles of knives, called dragon teeth, tiger wings, and dog gods. They were held by a strange man when his ancestors were selling boats. After many years, no one came back to redeem the sword. Later, he asked the Lu family to learn about the sword. It was said to contain demonic Qi and its power was extraordinary, but it was three devil sabers. There are predecessors in the ancestors who once used it, and soon it turns into devils. Yu Feng fell asleep after he said it, but Wu Xingzhi didn't care about what Yu Feng said after hearing it, so he sat in meditation and vigil. At the time of Yin, a passerby came crying from the north but saw a giant bear chasing after him. The bear was originally white, but its face was dyed red, its palms were as big as palm-leaf fans, and when it ran, it made a booming sound, and the ground trembled. Wu Xingzhi was in a hurry to call Yu Feng, and the two fought together, lost, and both were seriously injured. In a hurry, Yu Feng took the devil sword, and the two fought desperately, eventually hurting the white bear, and the white bear roared away. After this battle, the two felt that their cultivation was still shallow, so they decided to meet once a year, and compare the number of devils until one of them was convinced.

A few years later, Wu Xingzhi traveled to the three continents and achieved success in the five elements, while Yu Feng often rode horses to the north. The two of them would meet every year to have a drink and chat. Comparing the bets, each had its own outcome, but they were both unconvinced. However, Yu Feng is rebellious by nature, and he will save anyone in trouble. If Yu Feng is defeated, he will hold a devil sword and gradually fall into the devil's way. When Yu Feng's consciousness is about to be destroyed, he kills himself with the sword. When Wu heard the news, he stood outside the gate of Luxue City for a long time, until late at night, kowtowed and wept loudly. Afterward, Wu Xingzhi collected the devil sabers and handed them over to the Lu family. The three devil sabers were fused into one, and the sheath was made of black iron, sealed with inscription patterns so that the demonic Qi could not leak out, and it was called Settle the Storm.
