Story

First meet

Yan Xue, the eldest daughter of the Yan family in the eastern continent, it is said that due to the bloodline of her family, her soul was damaged, resulting in red in her hair. She is usually not talkative and has no other hobbies except practicing the sword. Before entering the Cloud Spirit sword sect, she held a blazing sword alone and the hostility is slightly heavier. Afterward, under the guidance of the suzerain, another Ice sword was used together with the fire-blazing sword to get balance. She also integrates the dance of her clan into sword moves, holding a sword and dancing like ghosts and gods.

Bloodline

The Yan family of the Dayan Kingdom was a famous family in ancient times. At that time, there was a lot of war in the north of the Dayan Kingdom and the Chiyou clan in the northeast was bewitched by the Zhuyan tribe of evil, so they started the war. Yan's family fought against Zhuyan at the east sea. After nine days, there were many dead and both sides were exhausted. And then Yan's family hurt Zhuyan, dances in their blood, and their momentum is terrifying attracting the golden crow from the west. The golden crow split its soul to help the Yan family and the Zhuyan was killed by the Yan family. Hit back the Chiyou clan to the north and surrender, the rest tribe all belong to the Dayan kingdom. After the war, Yan's family stands north of Dayan, serving in golden crow. Because during the battle they were covered with Zhuyan's blood so their soul was damaged. There is a lot of Yan's posterity that is soul damaged and needs the golden crow's split soul to survive.

There is hearsay in the three continents, the eldest daughter of the Yan family, Yan Xue, was born with huge damage to her soul. Even though the golden crow's soul fixed her soul, the Three Souls still cannot be intact. When she was 15 years old, there are some red hair appeared on her head, the elder in the family thought this could be the omen of the evil bloodline, so they sought treatment from Old Crane in Danding Peak. The Old Crane saw that she was talented and intelligent and her spiritual platform was clean and good at witch dance. It is very suited for practice swords. Hence he writes a letter to the Cloud Spirit Sword Sect.

Yan Xue affects by her bloodline, her sword will burst out in battle, and the more she fights, the braver you are, and there are very few ordinary cultivators who can parry. And practice the sect's dharma in the Cloud Spirit Sword Sect, her mind is as peaceful and lofty as clouds and mist. And her damaged soul can even help her to practice, so sword cultivation is far superior to the disciples of the same generation.

The Ice-Blazing Sword

The two swords of Yan Xue, one called drift ice and the other blazing fire are about three feet and two inches long. The drift ice one is blue and the blazing fire one is red. The blue one was forged by the ice stone in the Sword Graveyard of the sect and the red one was forged by the high-grade refined iron ore from the golden crow. Hold them, ice will protect the body and destroy the enemy with blazing fire.

When Yan Xue was young, she usually danced the witch dance with a blazing sword, obtained golden crow's Qi, and the path to cultivation was first revealed. And then she entered the Cloud Spirit Sword Sect, cultivate with a blazing sword. Although the moves are fierce and always defeated the enemy through their weakness. However, the fire's power can trigger her bloodline's power, which could easily make her have deviation syndrome. Thanks to the guidance of the suzerain of the sect, forged an ice sword to balance the effect of the fire and the power of her bloodline can be suppressed.

Yan Xue cultivate with two swords for several years, the two-spirit of ice and fire suppress each other and her innate bloodline tends to be stable. Although there are some occasional rumors that Yan Xue's eyes are blood red and fierce on her moves when she is cornered. But the suzerain said that after he observed her carefully, There is no sign of turning red in her hair, it is only necessary to practice more mental methods to purify the mind. During this period, Yan Xue devoted herself to Daoism. Whenever there was a sect mission, she would return within three days. The efficiency was high and the methods were sharp, making people speechless. After returning to the sect, she will go to the martial arena at once and practices her sword. The road of cultivation is getting farther and farther, and it seems that she has gotten rid of her bloodline's shackles.

Flame Dance

The Wu is the job that descends the gods through dancing. Yan's witch dance was originally the sacrificial dance of the Yan clan. After rehearsed by the patriarch, turning they dance a sword and turn it into a sword dance. In ancient times, when the people of the Yan clan fought against Zhu Yan, they all danced with swords that were magnificent.

When Yan Xue was a child, she was not like an ordinary child. She had a quiet temperament and didn't like to play with others. She always learned to perform witch dances in front of the ancestral hall all day long and when she danced to the point of forgetting her feelings, she always attracted the golden crow and filled with the Qi of the golden crow and got through the two veins of Ren and Du. The children of the clan saw Yan Xue get Qi very early, entering the immortal path at a such young age, not an ordinary person. Immortals and mortals have different paths, so those children leave Yan Xue as far as possible. Only her younger brother Yan Chen occasionally accompanied her in the witch dance. Yan Xue is not annoyed, she still dances all day long. After a long time, her dance seems to be natural and when she dances with a sword, she has the posture of her ancestors.

After joining the Cloud Spirit Sword Sect, when she practiced swords, she often danced into the sword moves. Her dancing posture was steady and straight, her steps changed naturally, and her figure was elegant and murderous. It is rumored that seeing Yan Xue's dance is like seeing the ghosts and gods coming to the world and wherever the figure goes, all evil spirits will be wiped out. For herself, dancing gestures are words, for her method of questioning, the dance of remnant souls, walking through the thorns of the world, asking the world and only seeking the righteousness in her heart.
