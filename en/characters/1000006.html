Story

First meet

Li Chengyun is the youngest daughter of the Li Clan in Qing Yan County, East Continent. She joined the Cloud Spirit Sword Sect at a young age, and learnt about the Ruiying Divine Sword's moves from the master's instructions. She was instructed by the Sect Master to supervise the training of the disciples.

By chance, she was able to obtain a good material from the immortals and forged a Yeying Sword. The sword's weight could be adjusted to its owner, and it consumed very little Qi to fly in the air. However, Cheng Yun is still young and small, so he is still a bit inconvenienced to use the Yeying with the same height. Fortunately, this year, she has grown half an inch taller, and her sword moves have improved slightly.

On weekdays Li Chengyun often travelled to Lianyun mountains with her sword, collecting the spiritual qi of the winds from all directions and improving her cultivation greatly. Because of its lively nature, often gossip with the disciples and disturb the sect's cultivation, often punished by the master.

Ruiying

In the Middle Ages, there were devils rampaging along the southern coast of the Western Continent, and the Lingshuang Mu clan defeat the devils, after that, they saw that there were mountains at the border of the Eastern and Western Continent, with a continuous sea of clouds and an abundance of spiritual qi, so they settled there to reject the devils. The surrounding scattered clans heard the trend and gathered, and began to live by the mountains.

At that time, the beast Yao became a devil, and its power was heavenly, sensing the gathering of spiritual qi in the southern part of the three continents, and it was attracted by it. After the beast went to the Lianyun Mountain Range, it ate countless living beings and swallowed all the earth, and the peaks of the mountain ranges were exhausted. When it was full, it spat out water to become a swamp, which stretched for hundreds of miles and stopped near the Flame Mountain in the west. Cultivators of the mountains were unable to defeat him and retreated to the peaks to avoid him. Cultivators fought with Yao for several days, and the master of the Mu clan fought to the end of his strength, and his spirit was broken and died, and the broken spirit pierced through the dome of the sky, which was cracked and there was a huge sword appeared, and it chopped Yao on the top of the peak, and sent down auspicious rains to wash and clear the poisonous swamps, and then the living beings in the mountains were free from worry. Afterwards, all the survivors worshipped Mu as their master, and worshiped the huge sword as the Ruiyin, thus the sword cultivator was reborn after the battle of the Heavenly Devil, and the Cloud Spirit Sword Sect was first formed.

After thousands of years, Ruiyin's Qi was dispersed and united with the mountains. Cloud Spirit Sword Sect cultivators built the Wujian Plank Road around the Ruiying Sword, and they would walk along the plank road on regular days to observe the Sword to understand the Dao. Some elders said that the Sword contains sword moves, which can only be obtained by observing it with all one's heart, but all the disciples often observe it without being able to do so. As time passed, there were fewer disciples to watch the sword.

Now there is a Qing Yan County Li's youngest daughter Li Chengyun, when she was young, she often listened to the elders in the clan said that the Mu's battle against the devil, the Mu's descendants of riding a sword to get rid of the devil, and the young girl's heart was attracted to, so Li chengyun worshiped in the Cloud Spirit Sword Sect at a young age.

When she first entered the Sword Sect, Chengyun was attracted by the divine Sword and sat on the plank road for several days without moving or eating. The disciples who watched were all aware that Chengyun was still young, and that they were afraid that her life would be in danger if she did not eat. A disciple advised, Chengyun eyes just gazed at the divine Sword, do not move the slightest bit, as if she had not heard anything, so the disciple reported to the head master. The master arrived, see Chengyun sitting on the plank, he ordered the sound silence, and his hand pinched the sword knack, so his Qi lightly touched the back of Chengyun's neck. Under the light point, Chengyun suddenly moved, shouted ‘I've got the Dao’, and said to surroundings that the Divine Sword contains thousands of strokes, and invite disciples to study together. The master was pleased, order Chengyun to supervise his disciples to participate in the study of the divine sword's sword moves.

Since then, although Chengyun was still young and her cultivation was insufficient, she integrated the sword strokes that she had learnt into her own sword strokes. After Chengyun began to instruct her fellow disciples, with the command given to her by the master, she often instructed her fellow disciples every time there was a deficiency, and she was always able to help them overcome it. At the beginning, the disciples felt that it was disgraceful to be taught by a child, but under the guidance of Chengyun, all the disciples' sword skills were greatly improved. Over time, Chengyun became a favourite among the disciples.

Yeying

One day, Chengyun was meditating on the Wujian plank road, suddenly she heard a misty voice beside her ear, said, the younger generation to undertake my will, all devils shell be found and killed. The sound is not over and the mountains shook,the divine sword suddenly shines golden, and there is a tendency to collapse. A short while, the sound stop. The disciples to find the golden light, see in front of Chengyun the divine sword bright light. The golden light of the flourish, attracted the elders arrived, the elders viewed also fear of its divine power and do not dare to go forward. At that time, the golden light is extremely, but everything has its counterpoint, while all the people pacing between the golden light, the golden light gathered into the Sword's body. In an instant, only heard the divine sword rumbling, a black stone from the divine sword crumbling out, straight down on the side of the Chengyun.

The crowd is shocked, do not dare to go forward. Chengyun was trembling, shaking and reaching out to touch the stone, but saw that the stone silently did not react. So all the people feel at ease, and want to transport the stone to the main hall to ask the head master to deal with it. However, all the people moved the stone, the stone did not move a bit. They also tried other methods, such as moving the stone with Qi or using magic crafts to collect it, but none of them move the stone. Chengyun was as anxious as fire, and she went up to hold the stone block, but she felt that the stone block was as light as nothing, and the people were amazed.

When Chengyun transported the stone to the main hall, shown it to the head master, the head master viewed it also felt surprised, said, since the immortal beheading the devil, it has been rumoured that the divine sword is not changed for thousands of years, and this time the divine sword is damaged, is it a warning to us that there are devils trying to invades the three continents? Then the head master wanted to collect the stone, but the stone was suspended by itself and hidden behind the back of Li Chengyun. The master laughed and said that this stone and Chengyun have immortal fate, so Chengyun need to receive it, and wait for it come to use.

The next day, Lu Jianxin heard the news and arrived, he wanted to bring the stone to the Golden Hut and cast it as a divine weapon. However, the stone is too heavy to carry, so Lu had to ask Chengyun to help him to move the stone to the golden hut. after the stone arrived, Lu Jianxin began to hammer and burn the stone, but the stone did not crumble or melt. Lu jianxin uses all sorts of forging techniques, but the stone has stay the same. While Lu Jianxin was annoyed, see Chengyun hammer the stone, the stone suddenly began to change, within a few hammers it has been as flat as a door plate. Jianxin was very happy, and taught Chengyun the method of sword casting from the side. Instantly gold hut fire light in the sky, the sound of gold and stone constantly. Only a few hours, the fire light closed, there is a black shadow broken hut and out. The black shadow shaped like a sword, hovering in the air, there is the sound of a tiger whistling and dragon roaring. Suddenly, there are thousands of swords come out of the sword pool, followed the shadow and fly in the air, its momentum is vast, swaying the top of the cloud, as if a dragon flying in the sky. A few moments, the black shadow fell and ten thousand swords return to the pool. Jianxin see this sword seems contain the power of the Immortals, so he named it Yeying, which refers to an immortal's weapon.

Yeying is bigger than other cultivators' swords, the length of the body is close to Chengyun, ordinary cultivators can't hold it, and don't know how many thousands of pounds it is. Chengyun can hold Yeying, because the divine weapon can recognise its owner. Chengyun do not feel the sword's weight, but because of its size, it will make Chengyun's move lost its dexterity, so she can't integrate the sword's moves into a coherent whole. However, when Chengyun tried to visit the Golden Hut to recast Yeying, it was already indestructible and did not move a bit.

Wind Rose

The Lianyun Mountain Range faces the cool winds of the Hundred Rivers in the east, the giant winds of the Canghai Sea in the south, the hot winds of the Lingguang in the west, and the cold winds of the North in the north. The mountain range gathers winds from all directions, and the qi changes in a thousand ways as yin and yang converge. However, the mountains are vast, but the disciples are scattered, the disciples often due to the residence of the qi is different, and the cultivation is far from each other. Among the mountains, the spiritual qi is mostly gathered in the main, cloud, spirit, sword four peaks, disciples need to ride the sword to go to cultivate, in order to eliminate the disturbance of the different spiritual qi.

While ordinary disciples ride their swords and consume a lot of spirit, Li Chengyun, with the help of Yeying, travelled between the mountains on the wind, consuming very little spirit. As a result, it was common for disciples to see black shadows travelling in the air to and from the disciples' residences, helping them to travel to and from the main peaks for training. Often this, every day and Chengyun chat with the disciples, the important things of the clan, the trail rumours, Chengyun hear, and passed on to others. Therefore, the rumors wind of the Lianyun Mountain Range, all collected in the Chengyun's ear.

Sect rumours are all collected in the ear, Cheng yun do not feel enough, and then she often come and go to the foothills to Tingfeng Hall. Whenever they have visitor disciples of other sects, Chengyun will be warmly welcome them, and exchange information with them. Over time, the three continents events, it is difficult to escape Li Chengyun's ears. At a time when the sect wind rumours, all the disciples heart dispersed and do not cultivate. The headmaster searched for the wind and caught Chengyun, punished her with closed-door thinking for three months.

Later, Chengyun often followed the winds of the mountains and cultivation, so the young body bearing the spirit of the Lianyun Mountain Range, and her cultivation to increase day by day. Thus Chengyun entry the sect for shot time, but her cultivation is more than ordinary disciples, plus the divine weapon, her reputation is as high as the sky for a while. As the saying goes, ‘A big tree attracts the wind and shakes the tree, and a person's name is a high name and a person's name is lost'. Over time, there are rumours that Li Chengyun was a bastard of the head master, so there are many benefits for her. There are also rumours, Li's intention to seek the Sect's Divine Weapon for a long time. Li Chengyun felt not annoyed by the rumours, but laughed and agreed, and boasted that Li's clan for the immortal bloodline, so there are always has divine weapons to identify Li clan as they master, in the future all devils shall be put to death by the Li's hand. Once this statement was made, the crowd burst into an uproar, those who believe, those who don't believe make a mess of noise. For a moment, the whole clan is like a whirlwind, in chaos. The head master sighed, and said, when the wind rises, the cloud surges, with this Wind resident, the Cloud is difficult to be peaceful again. So Chengyun was punished and closed for three months again.
