Story

First meet

Mu Hu, the posthumous son of a tiger spirit from Mount Tai, was raised by the people of Mu Village. Mu Hu ate spiritual herbs when he was young, and gathered Qi to transform into a demon when he was an adult. He has a pure nature and helps the village a lot. Mu Hu usually uses a Kun Wu Metal Ring, which is donated by the villagers and Mu Hu personally forges it. Mu Hu moved the golden ring with the power of the tiger, and when the ring moved with his palm, it was as powerful as a landslide.

The White Tiger Rudder of the Five Elements Alliance invites Mu Hu because he has a deep connection with cultivating immortals. After Mu Hu successfully mastered Metal Spirit, he transferred to Qilin Rudder. Mu Hu is usually good at meditation, and there are rumors that he can see Qilin while meditating. Mu Hu obtained Qilin's spirit of earth, and at the same time, he has a deep connection with Mount Tai, so his cultivation of earth spirit has improved rapidly. With Mu Hu's two spirits of soil and gold, few of his peers can match him.

Tiger of Mount Tai

Hundreds of miles westward from White Tiger Rudder of the Five Elements Alliance, near the middle of the Tai Mountains, there is a Kun Wu Mountain, where many mines are hidden. There is a village beside Kun Wu Mountain, called Mu Village. There are many metalworkers and stonemasons in the village, who make a living selling ore. At that time, the devil repeatedly attacked Mu Village, and the villagers were all mortals, irresistible, so they offered ore to White Tiger Rudder for protection. After that, practitioners are often stationed in the village to fight against the devil. If cultivators meet mortals who are predestined, they will also teach them some superficial methods of qi circulation. After many years, although none of the mortals in the village have become immortal cultivators, the number of mortals who can feel qi has gradually increased. Although the devils are fierce, they are no match for the crowd of qi refiners.

One day, a white-fronted tiger spirit with eye-catching eyes wandered around the entrance of the village, walking staggeringly, as if she had been wounded. The villagers took up hoes to drive her away, and her roar gradually became weaker. After a while, she leaned against the stone tablet at the entrance of the village and died of exhaustion. The villagers dissected her and saw a tiger cub in her belly. They felt sorry for him and couldn't bear to abandon him, so they worked together to raise him, hoping that he would return to the wild when he became an adult. Villagers fed this cub with goat milk, and in just three days he was as big as a grown-up cat, and was able to catch mice and snakes in the fields. He couldn't get enough of snakes, insects, rats and ants, so he started chewing spiritual herbs. Some villagers saw that he was born cute, and often fed him with spiritual herbs. The villagers gradually became familiar with the cub, and named him Mu Hu. The villagers usually call him Tiger, and Mu Hu always responds. After a few months, Mu Hu was as big as an adult man, and he stood on two feet like a human. The villagers were shocked and asked the cultivator urgently. When the cultivator saw it, he thought it was a tiger spirit turned into a demon, so he wanted to kill him. The villagers believed that Mu Hu was pure in nature, and that Mu Hu did not seem to have the intention of harming the village, so they protected Mu Hu so that the cultivator would not kill him. Since then, the children in the village have not been afraid to see the tiger standing on both feet. They often cling to Mu Hu's body and pull his fur. There are many children playing like this, but Mu Hu is not annoyed, and still plays with the children. After a few more months, Mu Hu's qi has achieved a small achievement, and he is about to transform into form. Seeing Mu Hu's facial hair gradually faded, looking like a human, the children in the village wept loudly. Mu Hu was shocked, and quickly returned to his original form, and the crying stopped. After that, Mu Hu no longer changed his form.

In the next few years, Mu Hu became taller and taller, walking upright, and his appearance was very majestic. Mu Hu often helps the villagers transport ore and resist the devil. Over time, he also learns the method of promoting Qi. A cultivator from the white tiger rudder saw that Muhu was quite destined to be a fairy, and invited him into the rudder. This was the beginning of Mu Hu's cultivator.

Kun Wu Metal Ring

There are many caves in the Kun Wu Mountain, and the ores from the three continents are mostly found here. Although the cave is deep, there are devils shuttling in the cave from time to time, often causing the rock wall to collapse, and there are many casualties among miners. When Mu Hu became an adult, he often worked with the villagers to mine. Every time there is a collapse, Mu Hu fights it with his body to protect the villagers. However, he resisted the stubborn stone with his flesh and blood, but he couldn't retreat completely, so he was often injured.

In order to thank Muhu for his kindness in protecting the mine, the villagers gathered the kun steel that the whole village had collected for many years and presented them to Muhu, hoping that he would cast them into armor to protect his body. Usually, Mu Hu often learns from the craftsmen in the village to make some objects. Although his palms are thick, they are very dexterous, and the playthings he makes are very popular among children. Now that Mu Hu has acquired Kun Steel, although he is not very clear about the method of forging protective gear, he seems to have an idea in his mind. Mu Hu wandered around the blacksmith shop in the village for a few days. He first transported carbon to control the fire, then he swung the hammer up and down, causing sparks to fly everywhere, and then he simply lifted his palms and twisted the steel with force, and finally forged the steel into a ring. Then Mu Hu embedded a tiger whisker inside the ring, and engraved the five-element mark on the surface, thus forming the Kun Wu Metal Ring.

Mu Hu wears the Kun Wu Metal Ring on his arm, the ring hangs on the arm without touching the fur, and Mu Hu transports the energy in the ring to form a golden body protection. Afterwards, Muhu used the ring to protect the miners, and they were never harmed again. In his spare time, Mu Hu seems to have learned the method of Duan Xuan Sect, hitting the stone with his palm, and after the stone is broken, the mine tunnel is connected, and the space gradually widens. Mu Hu also transported wood to set up a support in the mine, moved stones and built walls to separate the devils, and the mining became worry-free.

Since then, Mu Hu has mainly practiced the Metal spirit and the Earth spirit. Every time Mu Hu moves the qi, the qi is sent out from the ring, and its momentum is like a landslide. Where Mu Hu's palm reaches, it is like being swung with a hammer by an immortal.

Qilin

After Mu Hu became an adult, he helped the villagers, and the livelihood of the villagers gradually improved, and they were able to protect themselves without any worries. Seeing that Mu Hu is not like a mortal, the villagers hope that he can enter the sect of immortals, which is also the honor of Mu Village. Mu Hu then joined the White Tiger Rudder of the Five Elements Alliance. After a few years, the Five Elements Tree bears fruit, and Five Elements Alliance cultivators gather at the altar to celebrate. Qilin rudder's master saw that Mu Hu was born with a strong earth spirit, far surpassing his peers, which is really rare, so he forced Mu Hu to transfer to the rudder of Qilin. The White Tiger Rudder's master saw that The Qilin Rudder's master's will was hard to refuse, so he agreed to Mu Hu's transfer to Qilin Rudder. Mu Hu was born simple and easygoing, so he agreed.

The Qilin Rudder of the Five Elements Alliance is located at the foot of the Five Elements Mountain. Among the cultivators who often live in the Five Elements Mountain, there are more cultivators in the main rudder and fewer in the Qilin rudder. Because there are few cultivators under the rudder, the rudder master does not set up halls based on stars. There is only one Qilin hall on the whole rudder, and most of the disciples are taught by the rudder master himself. Since Mu Hu practiced in Qilin Rudder, the earth spirit has made great progress, and he is no longer at a disadvantage when he is fighting with the rudder master.

One day, when Mu Hu was practicing meditation, he vaguely saw a beast with a sheep's head, wolf's hooves and scales on its body. Because of the benevolence and righteousness in Mu Hu's body, the beast is very close to Mu Hu. The beast presented Mu Hu with scales, and the scales disappeared when they touched Mu Hu's palm. After the scales disappeared, the beast and Mu Hu communicated with each other. The beast passed the good fortune of the three continents earth spirit to Mu Hu, and Mu Hu combined the seal method in the rudder to cultivate, suddenly felt that the spiritual platform was clear, and he cultivated diligently. Mu Hu then passed it on to the rudder, and all fellow cultivators made progress. Later, Muhu often practiced meditation, and his relationship with the beast gradually deepened. The beast hoped that Mu Hu would do more benevolent and righteous things, so that he could see the Yellow Dragon and understand the way of heaven. Mu Hu was very surprised, but he didn't take it seriously, and did not change his daily behavior. Everyone only said that Mu Hu's nature is like this, and that doing more benevolence and righteousness is not for utilitarianism.
