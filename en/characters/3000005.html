Story

First meet

Nangong Sheng, the Westerner. His Westerner's name is Milad, and surname is unknown. Nangong Sheng encountered the West Continent Gu Insect Crisis when he was young and was saved by Vermilion Bird. Later, he was brought back to the Vermilion Bird Rudder by Master Nangong, and was given the name Nangong Sheng. Because of the original fire spirit given by Vermilion Bird, Nangong Sheng cultivated very fast and was quite talented in the five element seal method.

Nangong Sheng is mischievous by nature and full of justice. He is quite famous in the Lingguang country, very popular with the children of the states of the Lingguang country, and they gave him a nickname, called the little overlord of the Lingguang.

Vermilion Bird and Lingguang

Before the five elements cultivators gathered in the Five Elements Mountain to establish the alliance, the people of the West Continent were attracted by the QI of the Vermilion Bird, have come to the foothills of the Flaming Mountain. Flaming Mountain range stretches for thousands of miles, and is full of fire spirit Qi, the devils are afraid of the QI of the vermilion bird and do not dare to come to the Flaming Mountain.

The people who could sense the fire spirit qi settled at the foot of the Flaming Mountain and practiced for many years to become fire spirit cultivators. Later, the fire spirit cultivators joined the Five Elements Alliance and set up the Vermilion Bird Rudder in the Jinzhou. Usually, the Vermilion Bird rudder cultivators would set up altars in the neighboring states to recruit people with immortal destiny and pass on to them the skills of the five elements. The altars are always lit with the flame of fire spirits, which can help the people expel the demonic Qi. People who are infected with evil spirits can pay spirit stones to the fire spirit cultivators to save their lives.

Later the mortals recuperated, and with their numbers, they opened up the land and built cities along the Flaming Mountains. The cities were connected by roads across several states and gradually became the country of Lingguang. The Heaven's Eye Sect was established in the country to recruit people who could sense the Qi but did not reach the level of cultivation. After many years, the Heaven's Eye Sect has thousands of people, and is good at forming hundred-man formations, so that when the formations are opened, they can connect with the eyes of the Heavenly Path, so that they can see all the things in the territory of Lingguang. With the information of this formation, the administrators of Lingguang Country have kept the whole territory safe and secure.

After a thousand years, the cultivation of cultivators in the three continents was hindered by the shackles of the heavenly path. On the one hand, the cultivators are suffering from the lack of spiritual stones for their daily cultivation. On the other hand, the mortals want to live longer and need the help of the immortals. The two complemented each other, each take what they need, and everything remained peaceful.

Lingguang is famous for its peace and stability in the three continents, and many people come here with admiration. There were even some people from outside the three continents who came from the west side of Tai Mountain. These people who came over the mountains were called the Westerners. At the beginning, the westerners were very different from the people of the three continents in language and appearance. After many years, the westerners and the people of the three continents integrated, although there are still differences in appearance, but the language and behavior of the westerners are already very similar to the people of the three continents.

Nangong Sheng and his people are all Westerners.

Gu Insect Crisis

YiXian calendar, six WuSheng year, there is a demonic cultivator of Dark Valley, who stole the Gu Insects from Guzang Valley of WuGe Mountain, he infected the Gu Insects with demonic Qi and spread the Gu Insects in WuGe Mountain, hoping to destroy the Qi pattern of the West Continent. The Gu Insects passed through the Wuge Mountain, crossed the land of the demons, and went west to the foothills of the Tai Mountain, infecting countless living beings. At that time, both immortals and mortals were locked down and kept their flames indoors to protect themselves. If the living beings in the West Continent were infected with Gu Insects, they would either turn into devils or die of exhaustion. After a few months, the West Continent is full of wandering souls, which attracted the attention of the Vermilion Bird. The Vermilion Bird wandered around the West Continent for seven days, burning out all the Gu Insects, and all the souls were drawn by the Vermilion Bird and returned to Tai Mountain.

During that time, the vermilion bird flew to the flame mountain near, saw a lot of westerners lying in the wild, seems to have died for a long time, and surrounded by demonic Qi. The Vermilion Bird waved its wings and burned them, and all the souls came out. Vermilion Bird wanted to fly away, suddenly saw a child still has a breath of life, because there are souls to protect him, he was not swallowed by the flame. It turns out that the child's parents to protect their son with their souls, unwilling to leave. The vermilion bird was touched by it and pecked out the child's Gu Insect with its beak, and injected the original fire spirit, so the child's life was safe. Seeing the child safe, the child's parents' soul slowly drifted to the vermilion bird, the vermilion bird felt the pain of parting and sobbed tears, tears fell into the ball next to the child, and the ball glittered.

Vermilion Bird wandered for a long time before leaving, when the vermilion bird left, the demonic Qi of the west continent scattered. At that time, the Master of the Vermilion Bird Rudder, Nangong Da, who sensed the fullness of the fire spirit outside the Rudder, so he went out of the Rudder to investigate. Nangong ran hundreds of miles, to the temporary residence of the Westerners, only to find a child lying in the ashes, embracing a ball, the child's Qi like a monstrous flame, straight to the clouds. Nangong was very surprised, used spells to strengthen his vision, and took a good look at the child, only to find that the child was full of vermilion bird's Qi. Nangong sighed that this child is quite fortunate, even in such desperate circumstances can be turned into good fortune. So he took the child back to the rudder and named him Nangong Sheng.

The Little Overlord

Among the cultivators of the Five Elements Alliance, Wu Xingzhi is the best to learn all the five elements of the seal. However, no one has learned more than Nangong Sheng in terms of sophistication. There is a rumor that Nangong Sheng was four years old to cultivate the Qi, and five years old to master the fire spirit seal method, and six years old to teach the Alliance's cultivators what he had learned.

However, instead of saying that Nangong Sheng is teaching other cultivators, it is better to say that Nangong Sheng is using vermilion powder to scribble randomly and other cultivators are just watching. In just a few days, the walls, doors, pillars, windows and chairs of the rudder were all covered with Nangong Sheng's graffiti. The seals painted by Nangong Sheng are unimaginable, and the cultivators in the rudder were stunned when they first saw them. But then the cultivators meditated on it and actually got something out of it. Seeing that there was nowhere else to draw in the rudder, Nangong Sheng began to make a fuss in the rudder, and when his Qi rises, only the rudder master can pacify him. Over time, whenever the rudder master is in seclusion, Nangong Sheng will escape to the states of the Lingguang Country for entertainment.

When he first arrived at Jinzhou City, Nangong Sheng saw the jade statue of the vermilion bird in the city and felt quite close to it, so he rolled over and rode on the neck of the jade statue, like riding a bamboo horse, laughing and joking around. The Heaven's Eye Sect heard the news and wanted to arrest Nangong Sheng. Several people from the Heaven's Eye Sect used tactics to catch Nangong Sheng but failed, Nangong Sheng flew down, with a combination of fists and kicks, beat the people of the Heaven's Eye Sect to hold their heads and make a flee. A short while later, Nangong Sheng broke into the city school, and called several children, they came to the city market to build a bonfire, and dance around the bonfire. The sound of the kids' play attracted the people of the market, but when they saw that it was the child who hurt the Heaven's Eye Sect earlier, they were all afraid and did not dare to come forward. At the time of Shen, Nangong Sheng began to feel bored, so he walked out of the city gates with his head held high and went elsewhere.

The Heaven's Eye Sect sent an messenger to the Vermilion Bird Rudder to complain about what Nangong Sheng had done, and his words were impassioned and tearful. The rudder master summoned Nangong Sheng, the messenger immediately with a shameful look to meet Nangong Sheng, while the eyes with a look of fear. Nangong Sheng eyes like a torch, seems to emit fire, he looked at the messenger, the messenger immediately seized the door and went away in a flash. The helmsman saw this situation but did not blame Nangong Sheng, only and Nangong Sheng about the three rules, one shall not hurt mortals, the second shall not help the devil, the third shall not miss practice. The rudder master hopes that Nangong Sheng to bear the fire of the righteousness, sooner open his spiritual wisdom, and do not be devoured by the Yin fire.

After that, Nangong Sheng no longer bully the people of the Heaven's Eye Sect, usually just play with the children of the states. Every time Nangong Sheng came to the market, he gave toys to the children of each family, and the children all liked it and called Nangong Sheng their king. Nangong Sheng and all the children waved through the market, looking very powerful. When they see a treacherous person, they will tie him up right where he is, and tease him a bit, then hand him over to the Heaven's Eye Sect. Some time later, there was a child rhyme in the community that said, Jinzhou city, bonfire up. Burning the eye of heaven, riding the jade statue. Fist for the whip, fire for the horse. Group walking, under the bazaar. Vermilion tear, blue-eyed children. Lingguang state, little overlord. Bad people worry, devils fear. The way of heaven is clear, the Nangong return.
