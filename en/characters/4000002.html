Story

First meet

Tu Kui, the eldest disciple of Master Hengtong of Yang Shen Cave of Duan Xuan Sect. Born tall and powerful, excellent physical foundation, cultivated for many years with Master Hengtong, and there are few equals among the disciples. He is a brave and aggressive person, and if there is any disagreement, there will be a battle.

Tu Kui is very good at martial arts, and he can turn his blood into Qi around his body, and his punching style is strong and powerful, and his anger is infinite.

Fighting for bravery

There are many soldiers in the North Continent, and many people are martial arts enthusiasts. The North Wind Army set up the Scattered Clouds Camp in Luxue City to recruit people who were willing to join the army. In the arena of Scattered Clouds Camp, the winner of the match is appointed as the commander of the army or the commander of the quarter according to one's bravery. Tu Kui like to fight since childhood, his body shape is more than two feet longer than his peers, his peers do not dare to get close to him, so Tu Kui often look for elders to fight. To Tu Kui young age, his height is eight feet, his fist is as big as a bowl, his strength is as great as a thousand pounds, and there are no matches in the arena. The North Wind Army wanted to recruit Tu Kui to be their quartermaster, but Tu Kui refused. Seeing that there was no opponent in the city, Tu Kui often went out to the north of the city to look for beasts and monsters to fight with, yet he did not lose. The boss of the Iron Bone Sect learns of Tu Kui's courage and wants to invite him to be the head of the dart, but Tu Kui refuses and only occasionally helps him to carry the dart in exchange for some food.

One day, Tu Kui followed the dart into the Xuan Tie Mountain, to the center court, saw the Duan Xuan Sect disciples are all strong and muscular people, which is rare in his life. Tu Kui beckoned to call a cultivator to fight, that Duan Xuan Sect disciples see Tu Kui was rude, stepped forward with astral paces and flashed to the back of Tu Kui, lifted his lapel, casually threw, then Tu Kui was thrown out of the center court, rolling down along the mountain road a several feet before stopping. Tu Kui was first shocked and angry, and then happy, he was shocked at it's the first time in his life that he was restrained by other with only one move, and he was angry at the pain of rolling all the way down the mountain, but he was happy because after this fall, after a long time of searching for his own way, he is considered to have found the way today. So Tu Kui limp and return, shouting willing to choose to the most brave master in Duan Xuan Sect as his Immortal Master. Hearing this, the disciples of Duan Xuan Sect were shocked and blamed him for his arrogance. However, master Hengtong heard the news and arrived, observed Tu Kui as a talented candidate, even more appreciated his behavior, and gladly accepted him as his student.

So Tu Kui entered the Duan Xuan Sect and held the ceremony of opening a cave. When ordinary disciples open a cave, they take pride in opening a six-foot-square hole with their mortal bodies. But Tu Kui open cave, the sound of punches constantly for ten days, the size of the cave was ten times larger than that of ordinary disciples, is the largest of the sect's newly-opened caves. The masters of the 36 holes saw Tu Kui's fists, skin and flesh covered by blood, but his face looks joyful, so they said, Tu Kui's courage is not comparable.

Fist technique aficionado

Duan Xuan Sect's skills, long in the speed, martial arts, body and soul, the headmaster established the rules to order disciples to cultivate all the four elements, and none of them can be ignored. But Tu Kui only love martial arts, and take the fist technique as the honor, not bound by the rules of the sect, and cultivate in a partial way. And by the enlightenment of master Hengtong, Tu Kui, with his innate physique, inherited the rigidity of master Hengtong's fighting style. So Tu Kui's fists are so strong that there is no other disciple of the same generation who can match him.

Tu Kui usually like to fight, he once set up a ring in the center court between the twin peaks, fighting for several days, defeated all the good hands of each caves. Later, he fought with Ye Mingming of Changuang Cave, and they fought for several hours until the sun and moon alternated, and the day and night rotated, and it was difficult to distinguish between the winners and the losers. The sound of the two fighting, shaking the twin peaks, leading to the mountain top snow avalanche pouring, the ground trembling. Seeing that the center court would be destroyed, each cave masters step in to stop the fight.

Later Tu Kui set up another ring, no one answer to fight. Then Tu Kui boredom, summarize the experience of cultivating martial arts, writing a fist technique scripture, widely spread in the sect, the disciples have gained cultivation by practicing, the scripture said:

"The body is light, the Qi is swinging, the mind and body are perfect, the waist and the legs are united, the mind is concentrated, the attack is immediate, the reality is changing, and the Qi penetrates through the fists.

First punch teeth fall, second punch skull rotten, third punch Tai mountain end, fourth punch Canghai divided, fifth punch hell gate break, sixth punch ghost scattered, seven punch virtual space shattered, eighth punch ten thousand elements gathered, ninth punch haven door open, tenth punch Yang Shen descending..."

Yang Shen

In the sect, there are stories saying that the Yang Shen, is a Qi condensed from the void, this Yang Shen can be shaped when it is gathered, and become Qi when it is dispersed. Those who achieve it can abandon their shells and travel all over the three continents without leaving the cave. However, there is no one who can achieve the level of Yang Shen, nether the cultivators of Yangshen Cave nor cultivators from other caves. Even as high level as the masters Hengtong can only spread his Qi around himself, so it is even more difficult for them to talk about abandoning their shells and traveling to the three continents.

Those who practise in this way should be free from distractions and should meditate to realize the mysteries of the universe. Aggressive people such as Tu Kui, the disciples all commented that he was so hostile that it was difficult to meditate on his mind, and they were afraid that he would go to the devil in the future. Ordinary cultivators listen to this bad words, can be resolved between exhalations, but when Tu Kui listen to it, his anger rose from the liver, and his body flushed red, and he vowed to fight with the person who spoke nonsense to the death. In this case, the disciples usually avoid Tu Kui because they are afraid that if they say one more word, they will be punched and kicked by him.

Later, Xiao Bu spread the words, Tu Kui is not good at drinking, if he just drink the wine of the mortals is still a simple matter, but if he smells the wine of the Immortals, he will be drunk immediately, and if he drinks a mouthful of it, he will fall down in a second. It turns out that the wine of the immortal can help ordinary cultivators with their blood and Qi circulation, but when Tu Kui drinks, his blood and Qi are in chaos, and they will rushes to the head of Tu kui immediately, even if he has a strong body, he will not be able to do anything about it. Therefore, later if disciples see Tu Kui making trouble, they will splash him with immortal wine, and Tu Kui will fall down when he is splashed, so he often complains about his suffering.

Restrained by the immortal wine, Tu Kui only had to restrain his hostility, and he had no other preferences in his spare time, so he concentrated on practicing martial arts. As a result, he was not empowered by the Yang Shen, but realized the way of Xuan, and was close to the master Hengtong in terms of the depth of his meditative mind. After a few years, through countless battles, Tu Kui's fist technique reached a master level. When he was fighting, he could disperse his Qi in the surrounding area, and the color was crimson, as if it was transformed from hot blood. When Tu Kui punches, his feet step on the eight directions, and breathe Qi quickly to move, its sound like thunder, its momentum like fire, there is red Qi beside his body, the longer he fights the stronger he becomes, the momentum is infinite. Therefore, there are millions of ways to reach the Great Dao, and those who are able to do so should choose the one who is suitable for them and practice.
