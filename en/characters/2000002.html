Story

First meet

Yan Chen, Yan Xue's younger brother, who possesses demonic blood but has both the Three Souls and Seven Spirits, is a different kind in the clan. His hair was red when he was young, his personality was fiery, and he was far away from his peers. he is close to Yan Xue in the clan and often dances with her. Later, he entered the Heptastar Pavilion and was instructed by the pavilion master to draw blood with the buds of the five-element tree, and the hostility was slightly eliminated. Combined with the divination method in the pavilion, he also got the art of burning blood to attract thunder. He once visited Liangzhou and found a strange stone, which was struck by lightning to form an Ultimate Hexagram Base, which has the power to change fate against the fate.

The Bloodline

The Yan family of the Dayan Kingdom was a famous family in ancient times. At that time, there was a lot of war in the north of the Dayan Kingdom and the Chiyou clan in the northeast was bewitched by the Zhuyan tribe of evil, so they started the war. Yan's family fought against Zhuyan at the east sea. After nine days, there were many dead and both sides were exhausted. And then Yan's family hurt Zhuyan, dances in their blood, and their momentum is terrifying attracting the golden crow from the west. The golden crow split its soul to help the Yan family and the Zhuyan was killed by the Yan family. Hit back the Chiyou clan to the north and surrender, the rest tribe all belong to the Dayan kingdom. After the war, Yan's family stands in the north of Dayan, serving in golden crow. Because during the battle they were covered with Zhuyan's blood so their soul was damaged. There is a lot of Yan's posterity that is soul damaged and needs the golden crow's split soul to survive.

Chan Yan belongs to the Yan Clan. He is a different kind of existence. He possesses the blood of the devil, and his three souls and seven spirits are healthy. His hair is red when he was young, and his temper is as fierce as fire. If he feels unsatisfactory, he will be angry. For the same generation, he will fight with a fist; for elders, he uses finger points and glares at them with insulting words. The patriarch feared that he might be possessed by a demon, so he sought out a famous doctor to diagnose him. Not only is there no sign of being possessed by demons, but his body is stronger than ordinary people, and the people in the clan feel at ease. Normally, Yan Chen only feels that his peers in the clan are very boring, and he doesn't want to associate with them. But he likes to watch Yan Xue do witch dance which will make he calms down, so he often sits in the ancestral hall to watch the dance, and dances with Yan Xue from time to time, the clansmen think it is strange to see it, because both of them are lonely people, but they resonate with each other through dance.

When Yan Chen was 11 years old, Yan Xue join the Cloud Spirit Sword Sect, and Yan Chen hated Yan's clan even more. Later, Yan Chen said farewell to his family and traveled far away , till he join the Heptastar Pavilion.

Ultimate Hexagram Base

It is said that at the beginning of all things, the primordial world has not yet opened, and all things are chaos, and the chaos means Wuji.

YiXian calendar, six XinHai year, a flying stone came from chaos and landed on the east continent. And obtained by the Tiangong Workshop. They used hammers to smash it but with the small stone split from it, the shape of the stone did not change for seven days. They knew this stone can't be just an ordinary stone. It happened that Yan Chen was wandering in the Liangzhou market, and heard the hearsays of stone. When he arrived at Tiangong Workshop, he made divination for stone and knew that the fate of stone was endless. As he just want to take it back, he made divination again and found that fate was very dangerous and he had no choice but gave up. It was about his anger to break out because of the divination result, but he found it seems that there was Qi hidden in the surrounding broken stones. On closer inspection, there was black gold embedded in the broken stones. And then Yan Chen use his bloodline power and call the thunder to smash the stone. Yan Chen collected the black gold, dropped a bag of spirit stones, and left, leaving only the people in Tiangong Workshop staying in place for a long time, not daring to move.

After Yan Chen collected the black gold, he visited Lu's family, put the black gold in a furnace to smelt it into black iron, and then poured lightning into it. After a few days, it was put on the plate. This plate hangs with Yan Chen's movement of Qi, fixes the central palace to generate hexagrams, the hexagrams come from it, it cannot be broken, and it has the power to against fate.

Thunder Way

Since Yan Chen entered the Heptastar Pavilion, he has been arrogant and unrestrained when there are others around him. He regards the ethnic group as bondage, but in private, he restrains his character and devotes himself to cultivation. Try to find a way to solve the problem in Yan's family bloodline. After a few years, all the books in the library are read. So he traveled to three continents to seek solutions.

Later, under the guidance of the elders in the pavilion, the buds of the five-element tree were planted on the spine, and when they grew, the power of the blood was drawn to the wood, and the hostility gradually disappeared. The wood stands on the spine and brings fire on the wood, just like the Gnawing Bite in the hexagram. Whenever step on the Thunder place violent thunder will born from it and he can drive it with blood. Its speed is fast, its power is thunderous, and the violent thunder wipes out evil spirits everywhere.

Yan Chen often practiced this art of attracting thunder, and after a long time, he could draw the blood on the wood and form threads between his fingers, sending it as swiftly as thunder speed, when the spirits were struck, the soul would be shaken. And those who were not strong-willed would lose their minds in an instant.
