Story

First meet

Long Yao, the descendant of the Sea Dragon Clan, it was rumored that she was always blamed by her elder sister, so she ran away from home angrily, encountered Yan Xue, and then joined the Cloud Spirit Sword Sect. It is also said that a wound on her tail is inconvenient to walk after turning into a human form. So she often sits on the Sky Delicate Bracelet. Obtain inherited from the Dragon, it has the ability to call wind and rain, so that a blue sea coral sword can injure the enemy's spiritual consciousness. She usually hates fighting and likes to travel and play. Sometimes she is seen in a low mood as if she is worried about leaving home.

Sea Dragon Palace

Long before the minds of the creature in the three continents were not appear, when they were looking for an innate Qi, the dragon clan had obtained Qi for many years. They built palaces in the abyss of the sea, with carved railings and jade, shells, and pearl palaces, surrounded by corals and seaweed. Gap to avoid disturbance from foreigners. After the battle of the ancients, the sky was broken, the thick soil was creaked, the tides were raging, the Great Rule was broken, and the Qi was lost. Only the three continents stood at the end of the world. And the Dragon Palace survived near the three continents. However, the demonic energy pours from the gap in the Great Rules and is rare near the three continents. The Dragon Palace stands between the two kinds of power and the surrounding creatures have both demonic Qi and normal Qi. In the south sea of the three continents, countless monsters have been destroyed by the Sea Dragon Palace which guards the safety of the world.

After thousands of years, Ying and Yao were born after Yinglong. Ying inherited the Qi thousands of years and is extremely brave. She is also the current general of the Dragon Palace and often swims in the south sea in the shape of a real dragon to drive out the evils. And Yao is the second daughter and the Qi she received is relatively rare. When she was young, she went to the sea with her sister and injured her tail by evil. After that, she lived in the Dragon Palace and rarely came out and often transformed into human form.

For a long time, Ying blamed Yao for playing all day long and not seeking the right way. Yao couldn't bear the responsibility and left the Dragon Palace. When encountering Yan Xue, she admired her unrestrained determination, so she traveled with her to three continents and later worshiped in the Cloud Spirit Sword Sect.

Blue Sea Coral Sword

There is an immortal stone in the sea, which is called dream-weaving coral. This coral is an extraordinary thing that only exists in the dream of the dragon and disappears when the dream wakes up. One day, Long Yao had a nightmare and woke up in a panic holding the coral. When she woke up, the coral was not disappeared. It hung in the boudoir, with seven colors of light. Those who came close suddenly felt refreshed. The coral was extremely hard and could not be melted by fire and all attempts to make weapons are all failed.

After Long Yao entered the Cloud Spirit Sword Sect, Lu Jianxin attached black mud to the dream-weaving coral, burned it for nine days, and forged it into a sword. This sword is called the blue sea coral sword which was transformed by Long Yao's thoughts. When the sword intention rises, the sword will appear and when the sword intention disappears, the sword also disappears. It is rare to see Long Yao carrying the sword with her. When others are practicing swords with Long Yao, the coral sword often appears out of thin air, and when it is matched with objects, it can sense the spiritual sense and attack it. After a few rounds, its consciousness collapsed, and had no intention of fighting again. Looking at Long Yao at this time, there was no coral sword, only a smiling dragon girl remained.

Sky Delicate Bracelet

Dragons live longer than mortals, and they have had more artifacts for a long time. The year of birth of Long Yao's grandmother is unknown. She suffered from the disaster of the broken Great Rules. She cultivated to the phase of Incarnation and could not break through and died in depression. During her lifetime, she only loved Long Yao. So she presented her with a Sky Delicate Bracelet, hoping that her life will be safe and sound.

Sky Delicate Bracelets change according to the owner's idea, whether they are big or small, the big ones are more than building and the small ones are still tight between the fingers. The decorations on the bracelet also change in thousands of ways according to the Dao Mindset. If the owner's mind is clear, the whole body will be like jade or there will be lotus flowers blooming, or there will be a flash of golden light; Otherwise, there will be black or entangled with withered vines or red blood will ooze. Regardless of whether it is clear or turbid, it is extremely hard, wrapping around the body to protect the owner and sharp weapons cannot come close.

Long Yao seldom walks around due to tail injuries, so she often reduces the bracelet to the size of a human and rides it. When fighting swords with fellow cultivators, although Long Yao is not as agile as those with outstanding physical skills, she is not inferior to them in controlling the movement of the bracelet, and even with the protection of the bracelet, ordinary cultivators cannot hurt her in the slightest.
