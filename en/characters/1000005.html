Story

First meet

Lu Jianxin, the second son of the master of Lu's Villa, said to be at odds with the clan, away from the Villa to the Western Continent, joined the Cloud Spirit Sword Sect, usually little communication with masters and disciples. He is good at forging, and has a large forging furnace in the gold cottage where he lives, and can make a hundred kinds of weapons. Lu Jianxin's hundred weapons skills are proficient, compared to the ordinary cloud spirit sword sect disciples, he is strong and has profound Qi.

Lu Jianxin once traveled across three continents to collect the best materials for sword forging, and forged a Clear Heart Sword. Clear Heart Sword at first glance is just a sword embryo, but if someone started a fight with Lu Jianxin, the Clear Heart Sword will sometimes turn into sharp sword, sometimes into heavy weapon. Between a thousand changes, the opponents will often feel difficult to fight with Lu Jianxin.

Lu's Villa

In the northwest of North Continent there is a Pan Long Ridge, the ridge is like a dragon, the top of the mountain has a mouth, surrounded by some defiles, in the center of the Pan Long Ridge there is flat land, covering an area of several hectares. From time to time, earthquakes drive magma and dust to spurt out from the top of the mountain, and over the years, the climate of Pan Long Ridge is warm and the land is fertile. Thousands of years ago, there were Lu's exiles who came here and lived on the mountain for many years, and got a hundred acres of good land and countless livestock, gradually, the people flourished and Lu's Villa was built.

At the beginning, there are people throwing stones in the magma in the middle of the mountain. After the stone was put into the magma, it turned red and glowed, and seemed to be swallowed by the fire dragon, which swam to the foot of the mountain and spit out the stone into metal. The Lu clan cast the metal into tools, and later built a forging furnace several feet high against the mountain, producing many iron plows, shovels, scythes and hammers. Their forging technology is hundreds of years ahead of other clans in three continents.

During the past hundred years, Lu's forging skills have become famous in three continents. Successive owners of the village led their clan to build roads and vehicles, and by the time Lu Ziyan became the owner of the village, the village was connected to Luxue City in the east and KunWu Mountain in the west, so the commercial road was open. The Lu clan transported the metals of KunWu, forged them into tools, weapons and armors, and sold them to the three continents through Luxue City.

The number of devils in the North Island is more than other areas, the Lu clan merchants are often disturbed by them. At first, the clan hired the Iron Bone Sect to help, the disturbance has slowed down, but the goods are still damaged. Later, the lord of the Lu's Villa used the spirit stones as the fund, and sent the clan members to various immortal sects in the three continents to learn some rudimentary Qi methods. However, the Villa is full of sulfur gas, the use of ordinary cultivation methods here is very inefficient, therefore, the Villa is difficult to develop into an immortal door, so the Lu's disciples are mostly go to other immortal doors to cultivate. After a number of years, with the help of the immortal sects, the Lu clan gradually grew stable business, and forging skills have also improved, they are able to forge immortal weapons. Only a few years, the three continents top-quality weapons and armors mostly from the hands of the Lu clan.

YiXian calendar, six JiaWu year, , Lu Ziyan passed on the title of the head of the Villa to Lu Xiucu. Lu Xiucu had four children of the Jian generation, named Hun, Xin, Ming and Shuang. The eldest son, Lu Jianhun, was in charge of foreign affairs, the youngest son, Lu Jianming, was in charge of production, the second son, Lu Jianxin, trained in the Cloud Spirit Sword Sect, and the only daughter, Lu Jianshuang, practiced in the Moon Pavilion.

Desolate Peak Golden Hut

When Lu Jianxin first arrived at the Cloud Spirit Sword Sect, he often went to the martial arts arena to compete with his peers, but he was often distracted, with his eyes fixed on his opponent's sword, just not attacking, and his peers all found him arrogant. After a few months, Lu Jianxin no longer went to the martial arts arena, instead, he often stayed in the sword pool, and every time he wanted to go into the pool to fish for the sword at the bottom of the pool, he was stopped by the seniors. Seeing that the sect has no novelty, Lu Jianxin began to go out to travel, each time he returned, he returned straight to his hut, without any communication with other disciples.

A disciple on his rounds passed by Lu Jianxin's residence and saw that his bamboo hut was on fire and the grass and trees around it were all burnt out. The disciple helped Lu Jianxin douse the fire and then informed the seniors. The seniors advised Lu Jianxin to give up his distracting thoughts and concentrate on sword training. The peak he was living in now had lost its natural qi, so he had to choose another suitable peak. Lu Jianxin was not moved, and the seniors were not pleased. After a few months, and a disciple patrol the mountain, see Lu Jianxin bamboo hut replaced by stone hut, but also catch fire, and the stone hut was destroyed also, Lu Jianxin stood in front of the ruins, as if he was pondering. The disciple went forward to help, Lu Jianxin only waved his hand and refused. The disciple saw this peak already has nothing to destroy, so leave with resentment.

The next day, Lu Jianxin went back to the martial arts field, but did not bring his sword. He was carrying a large bag of other weapons and practicing them one by one. The disciples were angry and accused Lu Jianxin of repeatedly breaking the rules of the sect without repentance. Lu Jianxin carelessly arched his hand and invited fellow cultivators to come forward to contest. A fellow cultivator led his sword to strike at Lu Jianxin. When their weapons met, the cultivator felt a great shock in his hand, and lost the battle in a few rounds. The rest of the disciples were shocked and went forward to fight with Lu Jianxin. By the end of three hours, Lu Jianxin did not show any fatigue and even had the tendency to fight more and more courageously. The disciples were exhausted and had to form a sword formation to cooperate in the fight, and in a short time, all the weapons Lu Jianxin had brought were broken. Seeing that there were no weapons in his hands, Lu Jianxin nodded his head and said it was brilliant, and then turned around and left, leaving the disciples to look at each other with confusion.

After a few months, a disciple saw Lu Jianxin stone hut turned into a golden hut, the mountain peak where he lived has fire light from time to time, so the disciple called others to view it. It turns out that the golden hut is a big forging furnace, the golden hut inherited Lu's skill, when the furnace started fire, it could melt thousands of metal, and the night will become as bright as the day. When Lu Jianxin began to forge the sword, the sound of beating iron shakes the mountains and forests, all the birds were frightened and flew away. The forging apparatus in the gold lodge is all set, Lu Jianxin helps his cultivation with his skill of forging swords, and refines the skill of forging swords with the enlightenment of cultivation, both of them complement each other and make great progress. Thereafter, the sect swords are mostly by the hand of Lu Jianxin.

Clear Heart Sword Embryo

There is a rumor among the disciples of the Cloud Spirit Sword Sect that Lu Jianxin and his father do not get along well on a regular basis, so Lu Jianxin has not returned to the village to visit his family like an ordinary Lu for several years of training in the sect. There are also rumors, Lu Jianxin and his brother in the sword forging philosophy has a conflict. The Lu brothers advocate forging swords in quantity first, in order to meet the needs of the three continents, they often produce low-grade weapons and armor, of which the number of defective products are not a few. Whenever the Immortal Sect entrusted the Lu's to forge magic weapons, the Lu brothers had to give the work to the more experienced blacksmiths in the Villa. The difference is that Lu Jianxin forging sword advocates quality first, more like the experienced blacksmiths in the village, forging swords often months in seclusion. In another rumor, a clan disciple, claiming to have a good relationship with Lu Jianxin, he revealed that Lu Jianxin and the Lu clan actually have a good relationship, he often saw some Lu disciples come to the clan, visiting Lu Jianxin, each time they came, brought Lu Jianxin a large number of metals, spiritual wood, brocade, spiritual stones, immortal jade, spiritual pills, immortal herbs, which are too many to count.

However, Lu Jianxin's sword path, not as complicated as rumors, only one heart and one sword. Lu Jianxin like the sword when he was young, unlike ordinary children noisy and active, Lu Jianxin only sit quietly by the big forging furnace, watching the old blacksmith in the village forging swords. When Lu Jianxin was a little older, there was no weapon that he could not forge. Whenever Lu Jianxin made a weapon, he had to personally wield the weapon to test it. When Lu Jianxin wielded the weapon, it was as if he had been specializing in the skill of using the weapon for many years.

Later, Lu Jianxin joined the Cloud Spirit Sword Sect, he often went out to look for good materials that could be used for forging swords. For a few years, he collected purple jade basalt on the east coast, picked blue sparrow gold in the southern mountains, cut green fragrant wood in the west woodland, and took red flame glaze in the north snow mountain. After returning to the hut, Lu Jianxin threw the good material into the forging furnace, opened the furnace forging sword. At first, the material does not melt, Lu Jianxin use Qi to push the furnace to increase the fire power. When Lu Jianxin ran out of Qi, he began to put the spirit stone into the furnace, the burning spirit stone is numerous, the gold hut hot as purgatory, turbid Qi leakage cover cloud spirit sword sect mountain range, Qi scattered to attract the guardian spirits of the mountain to gather around. The master was reported, only said Lu Jianxin fate has this calamity, others must not interfere.

The burning continued for forty-nine days, the materials in the forging furnace began to melt together, Lu Jianxin busy filling materials into the sword Fan, only to see the material gradually changed into the shape of the sword, the hilt has been shaped, but the sword body is still red. Lu Jianxin began to hammer the sword body, but the sword body temperature does not drop but rise, Lu Jianxin was surprised, after thinking for a moment, Lu Jianxin used his own blood to complete the quenching of the sword, the sword got qi, the sword body began to take shape, the shaped sword embryo began to float beside Lu Jianxin. At this point, Lu Jianxin and the sword embryo were connected. The sword embryo would become sharp when Lu Jianxin's will was strong, and the sword body would become hot when Lu Jianxin became angry. One heart seeks one sword, one sword superior to ten thousand swords, this sword is named Clear Heart Sword.
