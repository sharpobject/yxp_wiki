Story

First meet

The mother of Du Lingyuan is the unparalleled beauty of three continents. When she was practicing in the Five Elements Alliance, an endless stream of people wanted to see her beauty. Later she gave birth to Du Lingyuan and didn't know Lingyuan's biological father, so Lingyuan followed her mother's surname. Inherited from her mother, Du Lingyuan also has the appearance of a celestial being. Every Five Elements Mountain holds a grand tree viewing ceremony. Among the visitors, few cultivators are not from the Five Elements Alliance, and most of them come to see her beauty.

One day, when Du Lingyuan was pacing under an umbrella under the five-element tree, a flower of the five elements fell between her eyebrows, just like a woman's flower makeup, and suddenly felt that the Qi was infused, and then stimulated the energy of the five-element primordial spirit. Holding an umbrella and using Qi to make a five-element formation, those who enter the formation will never escape again.

On weekdays, Du Lingyuan often treats people with a smile and behaves frivolously and charmingly. However, if there is a prodigal one who wants to be frivolous, he still has to ask the Supreme Spirit Formation of the Five Elements Alliance if it agrees.

Five Elements Flower Makeup

The sacred tree of the five elements on the Five Elements Mountain stands in the center of three continents, and the time of its birth is unreliable. Its branches are wood, the blossoms are fire, the flowers are soil, the soil grows the tree and the leaves become metal, and the metal leaves sway and become rain to form a pond. The Five Elements Alliance is divided into three continents, and the main rudder is set in Five Elements Mountain. An altar is set up to guard the Five Elements Tree. The tree grows for many years and produces world fruit. Every time there is fruit, the cultivator of the Five Elements Alliance gathers under the tree and opens the altar to discuss the Dao. There is also a competition for fighting skills, and the winner will get the world fruit.

It is said that the tree guard altar has been set up by the chief rudder in the past dynasties. There are restrictions, and no disciples are allowed to enter it usually. Du Lingyuan lived in the main rudder for a long time, and wanted to enter the altar, she only said she was happy with the scenery beside the five-element tree, and she acted coquettishly, and the gatekeeper couldn't stop her, so she entered, but the restriction was not triggered. The gatekeeper's disciple was very strange about this, so he reported it to the chief rudder. The chief rudder only said that Du Lingyuan was destined to come here, and they said just let her go with the flow.

One day, when Du Lingyuan was pacing under the five-element tree holding an umbrella, the flowers of the five elements fell on the head between her eyebrows. After the flowers are gone, only the imprint of plum blossoms remains, just like a woman's flower makeup, first there is a burning pain, and then Du Lingyuan suddenly feel the energy of the five elements in her body, which comes from the tree and has an endless Qi supply. Since then, Du Lingyuan possesses the Qi of the Five Elements. Whenever there is a battle, any type of Qi can be deployed in an instant.

Flowing Mandarin Duck Umbrella

Du Lingyuan usually hates dirty air, so she went to Tiangong Workshop to build an umbrella with clean air to protect her body. The outside circle of the umbrella surface is water, the inner circle is fire, the umbrella bone is metal, the umbrella handle is wood, and the umbrella fringe is made of jade, forming a mixed element. Usually, this umbrella protects against wind and rain, and when fighting with others, it can help Du Lingyuan attract the Qi of the five elements to form a formation in an instant, and use the umbrella to stimulate the formation. It can also draw Qi, and it can easily transform from protecting the body to hurting the enemy. It becomes a variety weapon.

However, when Du Lingyuan uses this umbrella, she likes to apply the five elements to overcome each other, urging them with the method of mutual overcome, and the Qi gathers to form a couple of mandarin ducks, which rotate with the umbrella. Gathering Qi to form, Du Lingyuan forced the Qi to do what it shouldn't do. Everyone was surprised to see her doing such things with half the effort and half the effort during her practice. However, Du Lingyuan only said: "I have few people in my mind, so I can form the same number of mandarin ducks on my umbrella. If someone can please me, then why not add a few more?" Everyone was in an uproar, but who knew the true meaning of this?

A Couple Mandarin Duck

The mother of Du Lingyuan is the unparalleled beauty of three continents. When she was practicing in the Five Elements Alliance, there was an endless stream of people who wanted to see her beauty. Du Lingyuan, the younger generation, didn't know who his biological father was. When Du Lingyuan was a little older, her mother ordered her to start practicing dharma, but her mother only watched the mandarin ducks by the sect's pool, and gradually became estranged. When Du Lingyuan was seven years old, it was freezing cold after winter, and the mandarin ducks all flew south in just one day. At night, Du Lingyuan saw that her mother had not returned, so she went to the pond to look for her, but she saw only a lonely pair of mandarin lanterns, and her mother was nowhere to be seen.

Until today, Du Lingyuan still does not understand why her mother abandoned her daughter, nor does she know her mother live or death. However, every time she sees the pair of mandarin ducks, she thinks of her mother, and her heart is extremely sad. Over the years, the abandonment by her mother has gradually become a demon in her heart. Du Lingyuan often uses Qi to transform mandarin ducks and applies the five elements to overcome as her will, but who knows that because of her innate vitality, she can also use it, which is more powerful than the ordinary five elements method.
