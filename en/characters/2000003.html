Story

First meet

Yao Ling, a hamster demon, was born in the dark but has a heart for the brilliance. She receives the light of the universe to obtain Qi, and cultivates the way of life to transform into a demon.

Yao Ling traveled all over the three continents to enter the immortal gate, but failed. One day she visited the Heptastar Pavilion, the master of the pavilion inherited the teachings of the immortals, and received them as disciples. She likes to hoard things usually, and she collects a lot of spirit stones and spirit grasses. The people in the pavilion see that she holds things and eats them every day, but they don't know what she eats. She is also good at business, she goes up and down with the group of pavilions and does some business to make a living. It is said that she may be the richest cultivator in the three continents.

In way of practice, Yao Ling thinks differently from ordinary people and develops a set of retrograde Trigrams methods by herself. It is hearsay that she can jump out of the three realms between retrograde Trigrams. Going to an unknown place in a short time, stealing a Red Yarn Lamp. When turning the lamp, and spreading it, there will be no disadvantages. It is also said that in the places she went to, the people she met were all dressed in strange clothes, and they often looked at the box in their hands. There was light in the box, and it seemed that there were thousands of worlds...

Rat Tribe

Rat tribes spread all over the three continents, but few can sense Qi. The Yao Ling is actually a different kind in the tribe. Rat tribes spread all over the three continents, but few of them can sense Qi. The Yao Ling is actually a different kind in the tribe. She is not afraid of light when they are born. She is often illuminated by the sun, moon, and stars, and gradually develops Qi. She transforms into a human in a hundred years and goes to the Western continent, traveling through the cities. She was learning people's language and daily life. After hundred years, she got smarter. To the east continent, eat what people remain. And sell the remaining items to others to start businesses. Use goods to trade with people. And hundred years later, she got a lot of money and started trading with cultivators and selling artifacts and herbs with them.

Yao Ling eats the elixir by herself, and her Qi gradually grows, but because she can't practice the Qi, she can't make further progress in her cultivation. Then she visited three continents' immortal sects to seek the method of practice, but those sects were unwilling to accept it because she is the rat who became a demon. Later, when she arrived at the Heptastar Pavilion, the master of the pavilion inherited the immortal precept of "Teaching Without Distinction" and received her as his disciple. Yao Ling has the art of Trigrams, only cultivation can lead her to the right way, and because of her heart and wisdom, she is born with the ability to invade the sun and the moon, and the road to practice will be unimpeded from then on.

Red Yarn Lamp

Ordinary Heptastar Pavilion disciples always practice a normal in the way of Trigrams, while Yao Ling is retrograde. Disciples of the same generation feared that it would damage her practice, and every time when they tried to persuade her, Yao Ling didn't take it seriously. She said cultivation is a way to go against fate, seize the good fortune of the sky and the earth, invade the mystery of the sun and the moon, and choose the right one for yourself.

One day, Yao Ling meditated in the pavilion and practiced the art of Trigrams retrogradely. After a while, she can see thousands of worlds changing in front of her eyes when she falls into a dream. When she looks closely, the changes will stop, and she can see people in gorgeous clothes in front of her, holding lamps and setting up altars, singing songs to celebrate, it is very lively. There are incense candles and jade silk on the altar, and there are five cauldrons that are used to cook food for the celebration, which attracts Yao Ling's covetousness. Seeing another Red Yarn Lamp, which is gorgeous and outstanding, Yao Ling is pleased, steals it and caresses it, and wakes up from the dream. Feeling regretful, she lowered her head and saw that it was the shining Red Yarn Lamp.

This Red Yarn Lamp seems to have the ability to attract Qi. Moving along the rotation, and drawing the surrounding Qi can help Yao Ling divine the hexagram. Moving against the rotation, the energy is attached to the flame, and the lamp is moved while the flame is flying, which can ignite the mind. Later, Yao Ling found a branch of the wisteria jade grape in the western continent and added it to Fengtou. The Qi induced can help the fruit grow, the Dantian will be filled, and the inner spirit will be clean after eating its fruit.

Business Wise

Yao Ling's wealth comes from extreme poverty. In the past when the stalls were first set up in the Liangzhou market, Yao Ling sold only fruits and vegetables discarded by restaurants, artemisias picked in the water, and fresh bamboo dug in the mountains and caught in the fields for weaving. In the beginning, no one patronized her store, only a few elderly people saw she was young in appearance, felt sorry about her fate, and helped to buy some commodities every day. After a few months, the wealth accumulated by Yao Ling was scattered in the village, and buy some handmade fabrics. She transported them to the city to sell, and the number of customers gradually increased. After a few years, she accumulated more and more, and she began to stock up on daily-use items and sold everything.

One day, Yao Ling saw a pocket at a pawn shop in the city. It was small and exquisite. She thought it could hold some snacks and carried it with her usually. The shopkeeper saw that it was just an ordinary cloth pocket and give it to her without collecting money, only saying that more business need to be done in the future. Yao Ling got the pocket and filled it with a lot of food, but there was no sign that the pocket was full. Put some more fruits and vegetables, there is still a lot of space in the pocket, Yao Ling was shocked, and then put tables, stools, screens, and vats, all of which were put into the bag leisurely without feeling the weight. It turns out that this object is a Qiankun pocket made by Tianji Valley, it seems to be from the hands of trainee disciples, it is low-grade. However, it was like a god's help when she was doing business, and she began to realize the wonder of the practitioner. Then spend a lot of money to buy spiritual stones, immortal herbs, and artifacts, and the guests will have cultivators. Yao Ling often gives discounts to cultivators, so she makes many friends.

When the Luo Chamber of Commerce heard that Yao Ling had made a fortune, it sent staff to visit her, seeking her way of doing business. Which said, Yao Ling also doesn't know how to do business, but after watching human beings get along for a long time, she can find that everything in the world can be traded, but the interpersonal relationship can't.
