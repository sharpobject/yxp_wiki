Story

First meet

Ye Mingming, the only daughter of the Ye clan in the North Continent's Mengze Village, was born with natural beauty, and was forced to get married by an official of the Lu Xue City. On the way to welcome the bride, the officer and his team, together with Ye Meiming's parents, were killed by devils. Ye Mingming escaped to Duan Xuan Sect with her natural strength and was accepted as a disciple by the master of ChanGuang Cave, Lihe.

After the death of Master Lihe, Ye Mingming's temperament changed drastically, and she went into madness, and was shunned by her fellow disciples. Ye inherited a set of practice method of restraining the toadlight from the Master Lihe, and her Agility is one of the best in the Sect.

The moon waxes and wanes

Ye Mingming was taken in by the master of Changuang Cave, Li He, who had mistakenly entered the Duan Xuan Sect by chance. When she first entered the sect, Mingming shut herself up for more than a month, and it was rumored that her arms and legs were broken and her mind was damaged, so she cried day and night, often weeping blood with her tears. The Master Lihe took pity on her, remodeled her body and temporarily sealed her sān shī, protecting her lungs with qi, so that Mingming passed through the door of death without any obstacles to her life.

After the shutdown, it was rumored among the disciples that Ye Mingming had gone crazy, and no one dared to get close to her at the beginning. After a few months, Mingming began to talk to the others, and they saw her moon eyebrow and cloud bun, flower cheeks and delicate face, listened to her melodious and melodious voice, and she often smiled and talked in a gentle manner, so the people gradually felt at ease. Mingming practiced with dedication, and with her innate divine power as the cornerstone, her cultivation improved greatly, gradually surpassing the ordinary people.

However, when the moon is waxes, it wanes, and when a man is fulfilled, he will be lost. After a few years of meditation, the master of Changuang Cave, Lihe, could not make any progress in her cultivation. Sensing that she was getting old and her life was about to be over, the Master Lihe summoned Mingming and passed her life's knowledge from mouth to ear, and then passed away.

The master has passed away, Mingming feels that there is no one to rely on in the three continents, and she is in mourning again. In addition, because of the death of her master, Mingming's sān shī seal was broken, and all kinds of emotions, like the night of the darkness, enveloped Mingming's whole body, which could not be avoided. So Mingming went insane again, and when she rose up, the sect was turned upside down, but after the masters of the caves made great efforts to stop her, the sect finally found peace.

Later on, Mingming was in seclusion for a few months, but when she came out of the cave, her hair was spread out, and there was blood sobbing at the edge of her eyes. When Mingming talked to people again, those who did not agree with her would get a taste of her sharp claws. She is so fast that even the disciples in the sect can't believe it. So get along for a lot of time, there are people rumors, Mingming's temperament changes with the moon, when the moon is waxing, her temperament is high, when the moon is waning, her temperament is low, if you have something to ask for Mingming, remember to avoid when the moon is waxing. As for the daytime, Mingming is often in seclusion, and it is even more rare to see her.

changuang

The void was vast, the moon was shining brightly, the snowy waves were tumbling, and the golden toads were spitting and shining.

In the ancient times, Changuang was born in the universe, started from the sun, and was carried by the moon. The master Lihe opened Changuang Cave to steal its vitality. At first, she chiseled a hole to draw Changuang into the cave, and practiced in the light, but got nothing. Later, she restrained his mind and watched Changuang being born in the Renal Yin, which flickered and disappeared, but she still couldn't get it. Over the years, master Lihe practiced facing the wall, and was able to stay in a state of constant tranquility, catching the Changuang with instantaneous luck every time he felt it. Occasionally, she succeeded in catching the Changuang in her dantian, and her cultivation was greatly improved.

Mingming has received the personal training from Lihe master, and she can also feel the Changuang. However, her mind becomes insane, which is far away from the state of tranquility, so she often senses Changuang, but she gets very little of it. Later, Mingming sense Changuang catch Changuang for many years, her reaction is more and more rapid, if there is a hint of Qi movement around, she must be the first to notice. Every time she fights with someone, when the opponent makes a move with Qi, Mingming feels that the movement was very slow, like a private ancient chinese school teacher shaking his head and reading out the moves. And Mingming was born with divine power, plus acquired the Sect's Agility enhancement, when she advances, she can break the move, when she retreats, she can dodge, it is as easy as turning over a palm. So when a disciple fights with Mingming, the disciple often does not even have a move, while Mingming is already finished the move first, and it seems to others that it is only a matter of moments, and the winner has already been divided. Therefore, it was rumored in the sect that Mingming was mad as a ghost because she had obtained the soul of Lihe master.
