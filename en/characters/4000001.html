Story

First meet

When she was a baby, she was adopted by the Zhenzheng master of Duan Xuan Sect, and named Xiao Bu.

Afterwards, Xiao Bu was given the right to take charge of the treasury by the Zhenzheng Master, and took charge of the disciples' living and cultivation everyday. Xiao Bu is intelligent but petty and gluttonous, and she is quite strict with the disciples. Xiao Bu uses a pair of tortoise rabbit censers, which can transform Qi into punches and kicks, and her moves and methods are so varied that no one can guard against her.

bolt of cloth

North continent in the ancient barbaric time, the tribes and demonic beasts are occasionally disputes, and yet remain peaceful. After a few years, the demonic Qi invaded the south, the demonic creatures ravaged the continent, and there were wars and chaos, resulting in a large number of displaced people. After that, the North Wind Army dispatched the Mountain Moving Camp to set up walls along the mountain ranges, and the walls stretched for thousands of miles, and whenever the devils attacked over the walls, the North Wind Army would unite with the Immortal Sects of the three continents to fight against them, and the North Continent was then at peace.

In recent years, the devil has been invading the south increasingly, and the army has been scattered, and the displaced people have risen again. One day, a couple of displaced persons, carrying their newborn baby, moved south, and encountered thieves on the way, and all their food and belongings were stolen. It was the coldest winter in the year, and the parents were afraid that the baby in their arms was getting weaker and weaker, and that the baby would die on the way to the south. After three days, the couple traveled to the outskirts of Xuan Tie Mountain, see the Iron Bone Sect dart team escorted the goods arrived, the two begged the head of the dart to accept the baby, in exchange for supplies to move south. The head of the dart team did not agree. That night, the husband and wife followed the dart team, see the end of the team carried miscellaneous goods with loose guard, so they quietly sneaked to the side of the goods basket, exchange the baby for a piece of coarse cloth, and then disappeared to the south.

The dart team arrived at the Duan Xuan Sect. When counting the goods, the people heard a burst of cries coming from the miscellaneous goods. At a closer look, it was the baby held by the couple. The head of the dart team reported to the master Zhenzheng of Puzhao Cave, who waved his hand and sent disciples to collect the baby in the treasury first and ordered the dart team to return to Lu Xue City the next day to find another family to adopt the baby.

Bookkeeper

Xiao Bu stole the elixir and grass to enlighten her spirit, and was adopted by the Zhenzheng master. Historically, when a disciple is accepted by Duan Xuan Sect, the disciple needs to smash a cave with their bare hands in the middle of their master's cave, and only then will they be accepted as a disciple, and they will be practicing in their own cave in the future. The Xiao Bu is young and her Qi is just developing, so she can't smash the rock of Xuan Tie Mountain. Master Zhengzheng could only place Xiao Bu in the sect's treasury. Usually master Zhenzheng only take Xiao Bu to check the financial records, walking around the sect to look after the disciples, but do not teach Xiao Bu cultivation method, so Xiao Bu gradually become an abnormal cultivator in the sect with no cave and no cultivation.

In a few years, master Zhenzheng thought Xiao Bu is older and she has enough knowledge to assume the responsibility, so he handed over all the rights of the treasury to Xiao Bu. Master Zhenzheng himself in the cave all day long to cultivate, no longer ask more about the affairs of the sect.

Later, the Duan Xuan Sect disciples have rumors that there are three major difficulties for entry-level disciples, one of which is to break open the stone and build a residence with one's bare hands, two of which is to cultivate with one's soul to learn about Xuan, and three of which is to endure the hunger and cold with one's mortal flesh. The first and second difficulties are the foundation of Duan Xuan Sect's cultivation, while the third difficulty is a man-made disaster. It turns out that since Xiao Bu was in power, was very precise in calculating the food and clothing for the disciples, each disciple was given supplies not more or less, but enough to go around. However, the Duan Xuan Sect has a large number of disciples who exercise their physical fitness, and those who are new to the sect consume even more, so they are often hungry and find it difficult to endure the cold of Xuan Tie Mountain. Whenever a disciple goes to the treasury to ask for food, he will be driven away by Xiao Bu on the grounds that all supplies are exhausted. Later, a disciple saw Xiao Bu eating the immortal food in the treasury and suspected that she stole the food for her own use. The disciple reported to master Zhengzheng, but the master only said that Xiao Bu was talented and intelligent, and that the distribution of goods was fair, and he did not care about it, so the dispute was closed.

Although Xiao Bu started late and her cultivation was still shallow, she was able to share the responsibility of master Zhaozheng with her young age. Over the years, Xiao Bu become more aggressive, bossy and demanding, the health of the caves, disciple practice, she is in charge of them all. When the disciples first saw being disciplined by a small child, they neither cried nor laughed, and all of them were unconvinced. However, after a few years, most of the disciples realized that their cultivation had been improved, and they began to look at Xiao Bu in a different perspective.

Tortoise rabbit censers

North continent Xuan Tie Shan, the peaks towering, pointing to the sky, the rocks are black, incomparable hardness, and is accompanied by boundless white snow, appearing to be mixed with the clear and turbid Qi, and the Qi is overflowing. Walking from the south to the north towards the mountain, only to see the Xuan Tie Mountain is divided into double peaks to the east and west, between the steep peaks have formed a chasm, like it's broken by the divine palm of the Immortals. Under the mountain, there are many mineral veins, producing many spirit stones. Duan Xuan Sect was established in the twin peaks, the spirit stone can be said to be inexhaustible. However, although there are a lot of spirit stones, but the mountain is less vegetation, and Duan Xuan Sect disciples respect the Master's order, shuttered all year round to cultivate, and food and clothing need to be darted by mortals from the Lu Xue City to transport. Mortal dart transportation capacity is limited, coupled with the North Continent in recent years of chaos, the sect's supplies have been sharply reduced, compared with other immortal doors, even appeared to be poor and downtrodden.

So Xiao Bu is usually bossy, mostly due to insufficient supplies, when she allocates each month, she tries to achieve a balance, as if she is tearing down the east wall to make up for the west wall. For years and years, Xiao Bu was busy managing the supplies and had no settled place to eat or sleep. Xiao Bu often calculates and eats at the same time in the treasury, and when other disciples saw her, they even said she was stealing from the treasury's stock. Moreover, Xiao Bu was often busy until late at night, and needed to light incense to refresh herself, and although she was young, she already had a head full of gray hair.

As for the way of cultivation, Xiao Bu is free and easy, unlike her usual accounting style. Relying on the authorization of Puzhao Cave, she can pass through all the caves without any obstacles. Xiao Bu sent the Guihu Cave disciples to fetch a thousand kilograms of spirit ores, half of the ores transported to the Dinglu Cave refined into a pair of censers, half of them sent to the Chaoyuan Cave to make a hundred bundles of incense, and put in the Wutu Cave to bathe in the sun and the moon, and ultimately Xiao Bu got a pair of Tortoise rabbit censers, burning the five elements incense. When burning incense, spiritual qi overflowing, Xiao Bu tied censers in both arms, walking in the sect, to purify the Qi. Master Zhenzheng asked Xiao Bu, why always walking, Xiao Bu replied, everything is for the two peaks of the realm to fill up spiritual qi, and it is the responsibility of Xiao Bu to be tired.

Unknown to others, Xiao Bu often used this Tortoise rabbit censers to make her cultivation twice as effective as the other half. In a few years, Xiao Bu has gained much in cultivation, and she can make punches and kicks with spiritual smoke, which is hard to defend, far beyond the ordinary disciples. With this magic weapon at her side, Xiao Bu can control everything in Duan Xuan Sect with her hands.
