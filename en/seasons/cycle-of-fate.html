How it works

  • In Round 1, choose one Fate Branch from three random options or the fixed option My Fate, My Choice.
  • My Fate, My Choice lets you refresh your Immortal Fate options once at each breakthrough. If you do not refresh, gain 1 Exchange Chance instead.
  • Branches can change Immortal Fate options, Daoist Rhyme Omens, card pools, and more. Some also change your starting Destiny.
  • Choose a sect below to see its branches alongside the general branches. Entries follow the order in the in-game catalog.
