Season Mechanic

Mirage

Round 1 offers a space-time vortex choice for a future move or another bonus; later, mirages recur every few rounds, affect all players, and present response options.

Mirage at a Glance

Choice catalogRound schedule
Opening choice

Round 1 always opens with Spacetime Vortex: 4 choices are shown from 24 candidates.

Regular roll pool

56 weighted mirages can appear by round window. When one appears, its cooldown blocks new mirages until the cooldown ends.

Rare branches

Low-weight mirages worth watching for:

  • Revisiting Xiaoyao Rounds 2-7
  • Revisiting Xiaoyao Rounds 6-8
  • Xiaoyao Divine Art Rounds 8-14
  • Revisiting Xiaoyao Rounds 15-18

Mirage Rules

  • Round 1 requires an immediate initial Mirage choice.
  • Later mirages are rolled by round window and weight, then start their cooldown after appearing.
  • Most mirages offer multiple options for different rewards, costs, or card-pool changes.

Mirage Choices

When a mirage appears, it does not necessarily show every possible choice. The number in the schedule is the number of choices offered; if the pool below is larger, that mirage offers a random subset.
