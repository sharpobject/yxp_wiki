Luck Season

Luck season adds four special sect cards for each sect. These cards belong to the seasonal mechanic, so they are kept separate from the normal sect card galleries.

Luck at a Glance

Pool shape

Four sects each receive exactly four seasonal sect cards, for 16 cards total.

Page placement

These are not normal sect cards, so sect pages link here instead of mixing them into the ordinary sect galleries.

Card pages

Each card still has its own detail page with all three levels and rendered card images.

Use cases

The cards extend existing sect engines: Cloud Sword, Astral Move, Five Elements seals, and Duan Xuan attack lines.
