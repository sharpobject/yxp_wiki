How it works

  • The Life Shop opens in Round 5. Spend Destiny to buy from four offers.
  • All players receive the same four commodities; sect and side job cards are randomized within the same phase.
  • Each round, one commodity receives a discount of about 25% (rounded). The discounted commodity is the same for all players.
  • The catalog shows base prices, before discounts. Buying a card also costs 1 Cultivation. You must retain at least 1 Destiny after a purchase.
  • Some offers require a minimum phase. Reward Scroll can only be bought after all other items are sold.

The catalog reflects the current retail game configuration for this mechanic.
