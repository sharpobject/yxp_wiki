Echo of the Dao

Season 6 adds a single Resonance Immortal Fate that can reshape a character, sect engine, or ordinary Immortal Fate for the rest of the game.

Resonance Immortal Fates

Opening offer

From round 1, you can inspect three random eligible Resonance Immortal Fates and choose one immediately or defer the choice.

Refreshes

You begin with five free refreshes. Each refresh replaces one selected slot. After those are spent, each refresh costs one Exchange Chance.

Eligible pool

Offers are drawn from the general pool plus entries for your sect and character.

Timing

Some effects begin at a named round or phase, and a few explicitly change according to when you confirm the choice. The individual description is authoritative.
