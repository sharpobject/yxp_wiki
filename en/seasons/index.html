Seasonal Mechanics

Rules, card pools, schedules, and choices for Yi Xian's season systems.

Fusion

Combine specified card pairs into fusion cards. Recipe pages show the ingredients and result.

  • Two named ingredients make each fusion card.
  • Result level follows the ingredients.
  • Fusion cards link back to their component cards.
Browse recipes

Dream

Dream cards change by phase instead of ordinary three-level upgrades.

  • List pages show the Incarnation version.
  • Detail pages compare every phase.
  • Dream cards are separated from normal sect and side-job card lists.
Compare phases

Mirage

A round-based event system that offers timed choices, rewards, and Mirage-only cards.

  • The overview lists rounds, weights, cooldowns, and choices.
  • Choice text links to specific cards where the reward is known.
  • Mirage cards are only one part of the mechanic.
Open schedule

Luck

Sect-luck cards are a season pool of four special cards for each sect.

  • Each sect has exactly four cards.
  • The cards are listed by sect rather than mixed into normal sect card pages.
  • Detail pages still show all ordinary card levels.
View sect pools

Echo of the Dao

Choose one Resonance Immortal Fate from a weighted pool of general, sect, and character-specific effects.

  • Three options are visible from the start of the game.
  • Five free refreshes each replace one option; later refreshes cost an Exchange Chance.
  • The catalog lists every enabled fate, its scope, timing, related fate, and offer weight.
Browse Resonance fates

Heavenly Derivation

Heavenly Derivation offers fate choices during runs and temporarily adds regular sect cards to each sect pool.

  • Fate choices appear at rounds 3, 6, and 9.
  • Some fates are restricted by character, side job, or round.
  • The listed regular sect cards are seasonal additions to the pool.
Open season

Limited Event Cards

Zongzi, Wish You Fortune, Battle of Horse, and other event-only card sets are tracked separately from season mechanics.
