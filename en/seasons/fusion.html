Season Mechanic

Fusion

Fusion lets players combine two specified hand cards into a new fusion card. The resulting level is the average of the two ingredients, rounded down.

Fusion Rules

  • Each fusion card has a specific two-card recipe.
  • Fusion cards are the resulting cards, not the recipe list itself.
  • Card pages show the recipe when known.

Relic mechanic

Furnace of Void

Card Storage: Can store 2 cards. When a card is placed, gain 1 Cultivation. When the card is removed, lose 1 Cultivation.
Essence Smelt: Regular sect card A + regular sect card B of the same Phase with a higher level than A = upgrade card A. Each round can only be used once.

The Furnace has no relic-specific fusion recipes; its unique mechanic upgrades cards instead.

Relic mechanic

Mirror of Stars

Stars Refresh: Each round randomly refreshes 3 sect cards, which can be locked to prevent refreshing.
Swap Star: Swap 1 hand card with 1 card from the Mirror of Stars. Each round can only be used once.

Relic mechanic

Pot of Xuanming


Pot Contains Water: Initially stores 3 "Xuanming Waters", unlocked during the Meditation Phase, Virtuoso Phase, and Incarnation Phase. After unlocking, you can drag your hand card onto the "Xuanming Water" to exchange them.
Xuanming Seal: The cards in the Pot of Xuanming will be temporarily sealed, meaning you won't get the same cards through regular card drawing or exchange. You can exchange hand card with the card in the Pot of Xuanming to change the sealed target.

Relic mechanic

Door of Kunlun

Location Exploration: Three locations are refreshed each round, and you can use the Door of Kunlun to explore them. After each exploration, you need to wait for several rounds of rest before the Door of Kunlun opens again.
Xuan-Yuan Artifact: Starting from the 15th round, the location "Crimson Star" will be refreshed. After exploring and obtaining the Crimson Star, you can fuse the Xuan-Yuan Artifact.
(The power from the Xuan-Yuan is unstable, and related cards cannot be upgraded)

Kunlun locations

When the Door is open, the server offers three personal locations. These are the 37 location definitions present in the client.

Personal. Three locations refresh in the next round if you wait. Choosing one applies it and closes the Door for that location's cooldown.

Mirage

Shared by the lobby. Waiting does not refresh it: the current Mirage remains available once per player during its 2–3-round window.

Values, card rewards, and cooldown length are supplied by the server with the individual offer; the client config contains the effect templates shown here.
