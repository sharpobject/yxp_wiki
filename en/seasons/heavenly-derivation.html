Heavenly Derivation

Heavenly Derivation adds a seasonal fate draft during runs and temporarily adds regular sect cards to each sect's card pool.

Fate Choices

Draft rounds

At rounds 3, 6, and 9, each player is offered 4 randomly chosen Heavenly Derivation fates and chooses 1.

Restrictions

Some fates are available only to specific characters, some only to specific side jobs, and some only in certain rounds.
